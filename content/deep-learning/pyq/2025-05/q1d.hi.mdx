**In simple words:** underfitting matlab model bahut kam seekhta hai; overfitting matlab bahut zyada seekhta hai (ratta maarta hai), to dono naye data par fail hote hain. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 93" />

**Underfitting:** model itna simple hai ki important patterns nahi seekh paata, to training aur testing dono par poor perform karta hai (high bias). **Overfitting:** model training data ko noise aur unnecessary details samet bahut achhe se seekh leta hai, to training par bahut achha par testing par poor (high variance). **Good fit:** sahi patterns seekhta hai aur dono par achha perform karta hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slides 93 and 94" />

<Img id="class-regularization-fit-curves" caption="Underfit (bahut simple), good fit (bilkul sahi) aur overfit (noise ko bhi fit karta hai)." />

**Fruit example:** underfitting apple ko banana bol deta hai. Overfitting seekhta hai "apple mein hamesha black spot hota hai", to bina spot wala naya apple "Not Apple" ban jaata hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 93" />

| | Underfitting | Good fit | Overfitting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model complexity | Too simple | Balanced | Too complex |
| Training accuracy | Low | High | Very high |
| Testing accuracy | Low | High | Low |
| Generalization | Poor | Excellent | Poor |

**Kaise pehchanein:** underfitting mein training aur validation loss dono high; overfitting mein training loss girta hai par validation loss badhta hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 95" /> **Overfitting ke causes:** complex model, zyada epochs, chhota dataset, noise. **Fixes:** zyada data, data augmentation, early stopping, dropout, L1/L2, simpler model. **Underfitting ke fixes:** zyada epochs, zyada complexity, better features. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 96" />

**Conclusion:** aim good fit hai, achhi generalization ke saath.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Underfitting learns too little, overfitting learns too much (it memorizes the training data)."
