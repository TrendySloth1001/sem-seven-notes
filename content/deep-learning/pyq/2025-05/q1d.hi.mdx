**In simple words:** overfitting tab hota hai jab network training data ko ratta maar leta hai, pattern seekhne ki jagah. Underfitting tab hota hai jab network ne pattern seekha hi nahi. Ek student socho jo ek paper ratta maar leta hai (overfitting), aur ek jisne bilkul padha hi nahi (underfitting).

**Overfitting.** Network training data ko, noise ke saath, itna close fit kar leta hai ki naye data pe bahut kharab karta hai. Training error low hota hai, validation error high hota hai (high variance).

**Underfitting.** Network bahut simple hai, ya bahut kam train hua hai, isliye pattern pakad nahi paata. Dono errors high hote hain (high bias). <Cite id="gfdl-ch5" loc="§5.2" />

**Pehchaan kaise karein.** Dono errors ko epochs ke against plot karo. Agar training error girta rahe aur validation error badhne lage, to network overfitting kar raha hai. Agar dono high rahen, to underfitting hai.

| | Underfitting | Good fit | Overfitting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Training error | High | Low | Very low |
| Validation error | High | Low | High |
| Fix | Bada model, lamba training | None | Zyada data, dropout, early stopping |

**Example:** ek spam filter jo training mein dekhe gaye ek sender ki har email ko spam maan leta hai, wo overfitting hai. <Cite id="gfg-overfitting" />

**Conclusion:** lakshya ek balance hai, jahan network training aur naye dono data pe accha kare. Is balance ke liye regularization sabse bada tool hai. <Cite id="gfdl-ch7" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "Overfitting means the model learns the training data too closely and fails on new data."
