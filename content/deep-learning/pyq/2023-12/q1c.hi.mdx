**In simple words:** dropout ka matlab hai ki jab network train ho raha hota hai, tab hum randomly kuch neurons switch off kar dete hain, taaki network kabhi bhi kisi ek neuron pe zyada depend na kare.

**Definition.** Dropout ek regularization method hai jo har training step pe randomly chune gaye neurons ko temporarily hata deta hai (unka output zero kar deta hai). <Cite id="srivastava2014" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai.** Bade networks overfit karte hain: neurons co-adapt karte hain, yaani ek dusre ki quirks pe depend karte hain, aur milke training data ko ratta maar lete hain, general patterns seekhne ki jagah.

**Ye kaise kaam karta hai**

1. Ek keep probability choose karo, jaise hidden layers ke liye 0.5.
2. Har training batch ke liye har neuron ko us probability se rakho aur baaki ko switch off karo; agla batch ek alag random set drop karta hai.
3. Test time pe saare neurons rakho aur weights ko keep probability se scale karo, taaki average signal training jaisa rahe (inverted dropout iski jagah training ke time scale up karta hai). <Cite id="d2l-dropout" />

**Ye overfitting kaise kam karta hai**

- Koi neuron particular neighbours pe rely nahi kar sakta, to har neuron ko aise features seekhne padte hain jo khud akele useful hon.
- Har step ek alag thinned network train karta hai; final network in sab ke average (ensemble) ki tarah behave karta hai, jo training data ki quirks ko cancel kar deta hai. <Cite id="gfdl-ch7" loc="§7.12" />

**Advantages:** sasta hai, zyada tar architectures ke saath kaam karta hai. **Disadvantage:** training ko converge hone mein zyada time lagta hai. <Cite id="gfg-dropout" />

**Conclusion:** dropout better generalize karne ka ek sasta aur widely used tareeka hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Dropout prevents co-adaptation of neurons and acts like an average of many thinned networks, which reduces overfitting."
