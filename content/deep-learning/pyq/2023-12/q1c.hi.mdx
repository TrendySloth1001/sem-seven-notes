**In simple words:** training ke dauran dropout kuch neurons ko randomly off kar deta hai, to koi neuron apne padosiyon par depend nahi kar sakta aur network training data ratta nahi maar sakta. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 107" />

**Definition:** dropout is a regularization technique that randomly turns OFF (drops) some neurons during training. Dropped neurons forward propagation, backpropagation ya weight updates mein hissa nahi lete. Har training iteration thoda alag network use karti hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 107" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai:** deep networks mein kuch neurons doosron par bahut dependent ho jaate hain. Agar ek neuron galat pattern seekhta hai to bahut se doosre bhi wahi seekhte hain, aur network training data memorize kar leta hai (overfitting). <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 107" />

<Img id="class-regularization-dropout-network" caption="Wahi network bina dropout aur training mein dropout ke saath (crossed neurons drop hue hain)." />

**Overfitting kaise solve karta hai:** random drop → less dependency → learn general patterns → overfitting reduces. Har neuron ko khud useful features seekhne padte hain, kyunki koi bhi neuron kabhi bhi drop ho sakta hai. Textbook ise bahut saare thinned networks ka ensemble maanta hai jo weights share karte hain. <Cite id="class-dl-m2" loc="p. 38" />

**Dropout rate (p):** kitne percent neurons OFF honge; hidden layers ke liye $p = 0.5$ sabse common hai. **Training versus testing:** training mein random neurons drop hote hain; testing mein saare neurons active. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 108" />

**Advantages:** overfitting kam, generalization better, neuron dependency rokta hai, network zyada robust, simple aur widely used. **Disadvantage:** training thodi slow, bahut high rate se underfitting ho sakti hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 108" />

**Conclusion:** dropout network ko kisi ek neuron par kam dependent banata hai, isliye wo unseen data par better generalize karta hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Dropout randomly turns off some neurons during training, so the network cannot depend on any single neuron."
