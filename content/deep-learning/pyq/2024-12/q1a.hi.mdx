**In simple words:** representation power ka matlab hai ki agar best weights mil jaayein to network kaun se functions express kar sakta hai. Ek perceptron bahut limited hai. Hidden layers wala MLP lagbhag koi bhi function express kar sakta hai.

**Ek perceptron kyun fail hota hai.** Ye ek hi seedhi line khinchta hai, to ye sirf linearly separable problems handle karta hai, jaise AND, OR aur NOT. XOR ko ye nahi kar sakta. <Cite id="minsky-papert-1969" />

**Hidden layers kaise help karti hain.** Har hidden neuron apni line khinchta hai, aur output neuron unhe combine karta hai. XOR ke liye do hidden neurons chahiye: ek OR nikaalta hai, ek AND, aur output "OR but not AND" pe fire karta hai.

**Universal approximation.** Ek hidden layer jisme sigmoid jaise neurons hon aur output linear ho, wo ek bounded region pe kisi bhi continuous function ko jitna chaho utna paas approximate kar sakta hai, bas utne neurons chahiye. <Cite id="hornik1989" /> <Cite id="gfdl-ch6" /> <Cite id="gfg-uat" />

**Limits.** Theorem ye nahi batata ki kitne neurons lagenge (ye bahut bade ho sakte hain), aur ye bhi nahi ki training sahi weights dhoondh legi. Same number of neurons mein, deep networks input ko ek bade wide layer se kahin zyada linear tukdon mein kaat sakte hain. <Cite id="montufar2014" />

**Conclusion.** MLP theory mein universal hai; practice mein hum deep aur moderately wide networks use karte hain.

Exam mein English mein likho: "An MLP with enough hidden neurons can approximate any continuous function."
