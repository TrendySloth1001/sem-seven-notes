**In simple words:** dropout ka matlab hai ki training ke dauran hum randomly kuch neurons band (switch off) karte hain, taaki network kisi ek neuron pe zyada depend na kare. <Cite id="srivastava2014" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai.** Bade networks overfit karte hain: neurons ek dusre pe depend karte hain aur training data ko ratta maar lete hain, general patterns seekhne ke bajaye.

**Kaise kaam karta hai**

1. Hidden layers ke liye drop rate chuno, jaise 0.5 (lagbhag aadhe neurons band).
2. Har training step mein neurons ka naya random set chuno aur unhe band karo.
3. Us batch pe patla (thinned) network train karo. Agle batch mein alag set band hoga.
4. Test ke time sab neurons on rakho, aur unke outputs ko scale karo taaki signal training jitna rahe. <Cite id="gfdl-ch7" />

**Fayde**

- Overfitting kam karta hai, bahut kam extra cost mein.
- Bahut saare chhote networks ke average jaisa kaam karta hai (ensemble).
- Har neuron ko khud useful features seekhne padte hain.

**Limitation:** training slow hoti hai, aur drop rate tune karna padta hai.

**Application:** image classifiers ki fully connected layers, jaise AlexNet mein. <Cite id="gfg-dropout" />

**Conclusion.** Dropout ek sasta tareeka hai network ko better generalize karne ka.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Dropout randomly switches off neurons during training to reduce overfitting."
