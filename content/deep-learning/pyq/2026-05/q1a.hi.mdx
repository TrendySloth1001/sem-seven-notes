**In simple words:** network ek guess karta hai aur loss batata hai ki wo kitna galat hai. Gradient descent wo step hai jo weights ko actually theek karta hai: loss par weights ko baar baar thoda neeche (downhill) le jaata hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 60" />

**Definition:** Gradient Descent (GD) ek optimization algorithm hai jo loss kam karne ke liye weights aur biases ko update karta hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 60" />

**Learning mein role:**

1. **Raasta dikhata hai:** loss sirf batata hai ki kitna error hai. Gradient (slope) batata hai ki kaunsa weight badalna hai, kis direction mein aur kitna. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 60" />
2. **Weights update karta hai:** $W_{new} = W_{old} - \eta \dfrac{\partial L}{\partial W}$, jahan $\eta$ learning rate hai. <Cite id="class-dl-chp2" loc="slide 63" />
3. **Repeat** karta hai jab tak loss minimum na ho jaaye, isse network apni galtiyon se seekhta hai.
4. **Variants** (batch, SGD, mini-batch) speed aur stability ka trade-off dete hain. <Cite id="class-dl-m1" loc="p. 34" />

**Conclusion:** gradient descent ke bina network apna error naap to sakta tha, par kam kabhi nahi kar sakta tha.
