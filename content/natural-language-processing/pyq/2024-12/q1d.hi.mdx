**In simple words:** bahut se words ke ek se zyada meanings (senses) hote hain. **Word sense disambiguation (WSD)** wo task hai jisme sentence ke hisaab se word ka sahi sense chuna jaata hai. Computer aas-paas ke words dekhta hai, jaise insaan dekhta hai. <Cite id="word-sense-disambiguation-slp3-I" loc="§I.4" /> <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-wiki-wsd" />

**Example.** Word *bank*:

- "He deposited money in the **bank**." matlab financial institution.
- "They sat on the river **bank**." matlab nadi ka kinara.

**Kyun zaroori hai:** machine translation (*bank* ke har sense ka Hindi word alag hai), search engines, question answering aur text classification, sabko sahi sense chahiye. <Cite id="word-sense-disambiguation-tp-wsd" />

**Approaches**

1. **Knowledge-based:** WordNet jaisi dictionary use karo. Lesk's algorithm wo sense chunta hai jiski definition sentence ke sabse zyada words se milti hai.
2. **Supervised:** sense-tagged sentences par classifier train karo.
3. **Semi-supervised:** kuch tagged examples se shuru karke badhao (Yarowsky).
4. **Unsupervised:** raw text se milte-julte contexts ko group karo. <Cite id="word-sense-disambiguation-gfg-wsd" />

**Difficulty:** dictionaries senses alag-alag tarah se baantti hain, aur tagged data banana mehnga hai.

**Conclusion:** WSD ambiguous word ka wo ek sense chunta hai jo context mein fit hota hai.
