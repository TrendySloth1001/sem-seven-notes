**In simple words:** NER finds the important names in a text, such as people, places, organizations and dates, and labels each with its type. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 35" />

**Definition:** Named Entity Recognition (NER) in NLP focuses on identifying and categorizing important information, known as entities, in text. Entities can be names of people, places, organizations, dates and so on. It turns unstructured text into structured information, which helps text summarization, knowledge graph creation and question answering. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 35" />

**Entity types:** person (Albert Einstein), organization (GeeksforGeeks), location (Paris), date and time (5th May 2025), quantity and percentage (50%). Context decides the type: "Amazon is expanding rapidly" is an organization; "The Amazon is the largest rainforest" is a location. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 36" />

<Img id="class-nlp-applications-ner-flow" caption="NER on a sentence: Barack Obama is labelled Person; USA, Honolulu and Hawaii are labelled Location." />

**Steps:** analyze the text, find sentence boundaries, tokenize and POS tag, detect and classify entities, train the model. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 37" />

**Methods:** lexicon-based (a dictionary of names), rule-based (pattern and context rules), machine learning (multi-class classification, CRF) and deep learning (embeddings with BiLSTM, CNN, Transformers). <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slides 38-39" />

**Conclusion:** NER converts raw text into labelled, structured information.
