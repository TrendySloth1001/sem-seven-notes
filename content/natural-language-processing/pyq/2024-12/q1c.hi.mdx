**In simple words:** discourse analysis text ke sentences ko jodta hai taaki poora text samajh aaye. Pragmatic analysis ye nikaalta hai ki situation mein speaker asal mein kya kehna chahta hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-asol" loc="p. 1" />

**Discourse Analysis** focuses on understanding how multiple sentences connect within a larger context. It ensures that the meaning of a text remains coherent and consistent across sentences and paragraphs. Key aspects: anaphora resolution aur contextual references. *Example:* "Taylor went to the store. She bought groceries." Yahan *She* matlab *Taylor*.

**Pragmatic Analysis** focuses on understanding the intended meaning of words and sentences by considering context, tone, and speaker intention beyond the literal meaning. Key tasks: intentions samajhna aur figurative meaning (idioms, metaphors). Ye sentiment analysis aur chatbots ko support karta hai. *Example:* "Can you pass the salt?" polite request hai, ability ka question nahi.

| | Discourse analysis | Pragmatic analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Looks at | sentences kaise jude hain | speaker ka matlab |
| Example | *She* = *Taylor* | "Can you pass the salt?" = request |

**Conclusion:** discourse analysis coherent text deta hai; pragmatic analysis uska asli meaning.
