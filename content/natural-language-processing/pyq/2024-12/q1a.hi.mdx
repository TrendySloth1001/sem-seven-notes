**In simple words:** har word ek word class (part of speech) mein aata hai. Kuch classes mein naye words judte rehte hain (open). Kuch classes grammar words ki chhoti, fixed list hoti hain (closed). POS tagging sentence ke har word ko uski class ka label deti hai. <Cite id="pyq-2024-12-wiki-pos" />

**Open aur closed classes**

| Point | Open class | Closed class |
|---|---|---|
| Matlab | Naye words aksar judte hain | Naye words bahut kam judte hain |
| Size | Bada | Chhota, fixed |
| Examples | Noun, verb, adjective, adverb, interjection | Preposition, pronoun, determiner, conjunction, auxiliary, particle |
| Naye word ka example | *selfie* (noun), *to google* (verb) | Koi naya "the" ya "of" nahi banata |
| Role | Meaning dete hain (content words) | Grammar dete hain (function words) |

**Tags kaise assign hote hain** (Penn Treebank tags). <Cite id="pos-tagging-ptb-tags" />

1. Sentence ko words mein todo.
2. Dictionary se har word ke possible tags dekho.
3. Aas-paas ke words se ek tag chuno (rules ya trained tagger). Jaise *help* verb bhi ho sakta hai aur noun bhi, par *provide* (jise object chahiye) ke baad ye noun hai.

| Word | Possible tags | Chosen tag | Kyun |
|---|---|---|---|
| He | PRP | **PRP** (pronoun) | closed class, ek hi choice |
| used | VBD, VBN | **VBD** (past verb) | sentence ka verb ("used to" = past habit) |
| to | TO | **TO** | word *to* ko hamesha TO milta hai |
| provide | VB | **VB** (base verb) | *to* ke baad aata hai |
| help | NN, VB | **NN** (noun) | *provide* ka object |
| to | TO | **TO** | |
| other | JJ | **JJ** (adjective) | *people* ko describe karta hai |
| people | NNS | **NNS** (plural noun) | irregular plural |

**Result:** He/PRP used/VBD to/TO provide/VB help/NN to/TO other/JJ people/NNS. <Cite id="pos-tagging-gfg-pos" /> <Cite id="pos-tagging-nltk-ch5" />

**Conclusion:** open classes meaning rakhti hain aur badhti rehti hain; closed classes grammar rakhti hain; tagger context dekh kar word ke possible tags mein se ek chunta hai.
