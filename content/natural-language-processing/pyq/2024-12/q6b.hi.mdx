**In simple words:** Named Entity Recognition (NER) text mein asli cheezon ke naam dhoondhta hai aur unka type label karta hai, jaise person, place, organisation ya date. (Paper mein "resolution" likha hai; is task ka standard naam recognition hai.) <Cite id="pyq-2024-12-wiki-ner" />

**Example.** "Sundar Pichai visited Mumbai on 5 May 2024 to meet Tata Group."

| Text | Entity type |
|---|---|
| Sundar Pichai | PERSON |
| Mumbai | LOCATION |
| 5 May 2024 | DATE |
| Tata Group | ORGANISATION |

**Steps:** (1) text ko tokenize karo aur har word ka part of speech tag karo; (2) dhoondho ki kaun se words milkar entity banate hain; (3) context se har entity ko type do. Context important hai: "Amazon is expanding" organisation hai, "the Amazon is a rainforest" location hai. <Cite id="pyq-2024-12-gfg-ner" />

**Methods:** rule-based (dictionaries, patterns), machine learning (HMM, CRF) aur deep learning (BiLSTM, BERT). Labels aksar BIO form mein likhe jaate hain (B = begin, I = inside, O = entity ke bahar).

**Applications:** information extraction, search, question answering, news tagging, resume parsing. **Difficulties:** naamon ki ambiguity, naye naam, spelling variants.

**Conclusion:** NER plain text ko labelled names mein badalta hai, jo information extraction ka key step hai.
