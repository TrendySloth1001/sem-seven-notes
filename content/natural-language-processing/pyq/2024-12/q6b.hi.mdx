**In simple words:** NER text ke important names, jaise people, places, organizations aur dates, dhoondhta hai aur har ko uske type ka label deta hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 35" />

**Definition:** Named Entity Recognition (NER) in NLP focuses on identifying and categorizing important information, known as entities, in text. Entities people, places, organizations, dates waghera ke names ho sakte hain. Ye unstructured text ko structured information banata hai, jo text summarization, knowledge graph creation aur question answering mein madad karta hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 35" />

**Entity types:** person (Albert Einstein), organization (GeeksforGeeks), location (Paris), date aur time (5th May 2025), quantity aur percentage (50%). Type context decide karta hai: "Amazon is expanding rapidly" organization hai; "The Amazon is the largest rainforest" location hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 36" />

<Img id="class-nlp-applications-ner-flow" caption="Sentence pe NER: Barack Obama ko Person label; USA, Honolulu aur Hawaii ko Location label." />

**Steps:** text analyze karo, sentence boundaries dhoondho, tokenize aur POS tag karo, entities detect aur classify karo, model train aur refine karo, aur naye contexts mein adapt karo. <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slide 37" />

**Methods:** lexicon-based (names ki dictionary), rule-based (pattern aur context rules), machine learning (multi-class classification, CRF) aur deep learning (embeddings ke saath BiLSTM, CNN, Transformers). <Cite id="class-nlp-m6" loc="slides 38-39" />

**Conclusion:** NER raw text ko labelled, structured information mein badalta hai.
