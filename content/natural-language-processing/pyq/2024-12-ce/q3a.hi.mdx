**In simple words:** bigram HMM tagger mein do tarah ki probabilities hoti hain. **Transition** probability $P(t_i \mid t_{i-1})$ batati hai ki pichle tag ke baad ye tag kitna likely hai. **Emission** probability $P(w \mid t)$ batati hai ki ek tag kitna likely hai ki wo ye word banaye. Dono tagged corpus mein counting se nikalte hain. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 7" />

$$
P(t_i \mid t_{i-1}) = \frac{C(t_{i-1}, t_i)}{C(t_{i-1})}, \qquad P(w_i \mid t_i) = \frac{C(t_i, w_i)}{C(t_i)}
$$

**Part 1: corpus se probabilities.**

<Sum>

<Given>

teen tagged sentences, har ek $\langle s\rangle$ aur $\langle /s\rangle$ ke beech. Tag sequences: (1) DT NN V DT NN, (2) DT NN V RB, (3) DT NN V RB. Words: a, dog, chases, a, cat / the, dog, barks, loudly / a, cat, runs, fast.

</Given>

<Find>

saari transition aur emission probabilities.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Tag counts.** $\langle s\rangle = 3$, DT $= 2+1+1 = 4$, NN $= 2+1+1 = 4$, V $= 3$, RB $= 2$. *Reason: teen sentences mein har tag gino.*
2. **Transitions.** $\langle s\rangle \to$ DT $3/3 = 1$ hai. DT $\to$ NN $4/4 = 1$ hai. NN ke baad V aata hai "dog chases", "dog barks" aur "cat runs" mein (3 baar) aur $\langle /s\rangle$ ek baar (sentence 1 ka last cat), to NN $\to$ V $= 3/4$ aur NN $\to \langle /s\rangle = 1/4$. V ke baad DT ek baar aur RB do baar aata hai, to V $\to$ DT $= 1/3$ aur V $\to$ RB $= 2/3$. RB $\to \langle /s\rangle = 2/2 = 1$.
3. **Emissions.** DT: *a* 3 baar aur *the* ek baar, to $3/4$ aur $1/4$. NN: *dog* do baar aur *cat* do baar, to $1/2$ each. V: *chases*, *barks*, *runs* ek-ek baar, to $1/3$ each. RB: *loudly* aur *fast* ek-ek baar, to $1/2$ each.

</Steps>

<Answer>

| From | To | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| $\langle s\rangle$ | DT | 1 |
| DT | NN | 1 |
| NN | V | 3/4 |
| NN | $\langle /s\rangle$ | 1/4 |
| V | DT | 1/3 |
| V | RB | 2/3 |
| RB | $\langle /s\rangle$ | 1 |

| Tag | Word probabilities |
|---|---|
| DT | a 3/4, the 1/4 |
| NN | dog 1/2, cat 1/2 |
| V | chases 1/3, barks 1/3, runs 1/3 |
| RB | loudly 1/2, fast 1/2 |

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Har row ka sum 1 hona chahiye: NN row $3/4 + 1/4 = 1$; V row $1/3 + 2/3 = 1$; DT emissions $3/4 + 1/4 = 1$; V emissions $3 \times 1/3 = 1$. Saath mein tag counts ka sum $4+4+3+2 = 13$ hai, jo tagged words ki ginti hai ($5+4+4$).

</Box>

</Sum>

**Part 2: "The cat chases the dog" ka Viterbi decoding.** Assumptions: words ko lowercase karo aur full stop ignore karo. Viterbi value $v_t(j)$ word $t$ pe tag $j$ pe khatam hone wale kisi bhi tag path ki best probability hai. 

$$
v_t(j) = \max_i \; v_{t-1}(i) \cdot P(j \mid i) \cdot P(w_t \mid j)
$$

<Sum>

<Given>

upar ki transition aur emission tables; words: the, cat, chases, the, dog.

</Given>

<Find>

sabse probable tag sequence aur uski probability.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Kaunse tags possible hain.** *the* sirf DT emit karta hai; *cat* aur *dog* sirf NN; *chases* sirf V. Baaki tags ka emission 0 hai, to unki Viterbi value 0 hai.
2. **the:** $v_1(\text{DT}) = P(\text{DT}\mid\langle s\rangle)\,P(\text{the}\mid\text{DT}) = 1 \times 1/4 = 0.25$.
3. **cat:** $v_2(\text{NN}) = 0.25 \times 1 \times 1/2 = 0.125$.
4. **chases:** $v_3(\text{V}) = 0.125 \times 3/4 \times 1/3 = 0.03125$.
5. **the:** $v_4(\text{DT}) = 0.03125 \times 1/3 \times 1/4 = 1/384 \approx 0.002604$.
6. **dog:** $v_5(\text{NN}) = (1/384) \times 1 \times 1/2 = 1/768 \approx 0.001302$.
7. **End:** $P(\langle /s\rangle \mid \text{NN}) = 1/4$ se multiply karo to $1/3072 \approx 0.000326$ milta hai. *Reason: sentence last tag ke baad end hona chahiye.*

</Steps>

<Answer>

tags: The/DT cat/NN chases/V the/DT dog/NN. Probability $= 1/3072 \approx 3.26 \times 10^{-4}$ (ya end symbol ke bina $1/768 \approx 1.30 \times 10^{-3}$).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Saare gyarah factors ek line mein multiply karo: numerators $1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot3\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1 = 3$; denominators $4\cdot2\cdot4\cdot3\cdot3\cdot4\cdot2\cdot4 = 9216$. To $3/9216 = 1/3072$, wahi aaya. Har word ka sirf ek possible tag hai, isliye sirf ek path hai, aur maximum wahi path hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

| chases | 0 | 0 | 0.03125 | 0 |

**Conclusion:** counting se HMM tables milte hain, aur Viterbi best tag path chunta hai; yahan har word ka ek hi possible tag hai, isliye answer DT NN V DT NN hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Transition probability is C(ti-1, ti) / C(ti-1) and emission probability is C(ti, wi) / C(ti); Viterbi gives the best tag path."
