**In simple words:** ek text mein wahi person ya cheez kai baar alag-alag tareeke se aati hai ("Ravi", "he", "the boy"). Reference resolution ka kaam hai ye pata lagana ki kaunse mentions ek hi cheez ko point kar rahe hain. <Cite id="discourse-reference-slp3-coref" />

**Key terms**

- **Referring expression:** wo phrase jo kisi cheez ko point karta hai (*he*, *the boy*).
- **Referent:** asli person ya cheez jisko wo point karta hai (Ravi).
- **Anaphora:** wo word jo pehle aaye word ko point karta hai. "Ravi lost his pen" mein *his*, Ravi (**antecedent**) ko point karta hai. <Cite id="discourse-reference-wiki-coref" />
- **Cataphora:** aage aane wale word ko point karta hai ("When **he** arrived, Ravi smiled"). <Cite id="discourse-reference-wiki-cataphora" />

**Referring expressions ke types:** pronouns (*he, it*), definite noun phrases (*the boy*), demonstratives (*this, that*), proper names, aur indefinite phrases (*a boy*, jo kuch naya introduce karta hai).

**Pronoun resolve karne ke clues**

1. Number, gender aur person ka agreement.
2. Syntax: same clause ka pronoun aam taur pe subject ko refer nahi karta, jab tak reflexive na ho.
3. Recency: paas wale candidates ko preference milti hai.
4. Subject preference aur salience: kahani ka topic likely hota hai.
5. Meaning: "The man ate the **apple**; it was sweet" mein *it* ka matlab *apple* hai, *man* nahi.

**Approaches:** rule-based (Hobbs' algorithm, Centering) aur machine learning jo har candidate ko score karti hai. <Cite id="anaphora-resolution-ibm-coref" /> <Cite id="anaphora-resolution-tp-nlp" />

**Uses:** summarization, question answering, translation, information extraction.

**Conclusion:** reference resolution agreement, syntax, recency aur meaning use karke har mention ko uske referent se jodta hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Reference resolution is the task of finding all expressions that refer to the same entity."
