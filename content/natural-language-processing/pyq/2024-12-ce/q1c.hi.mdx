**In simple words:** rule-based machine translation (RBMT) system language experts ke likhe hue grammar rules aur bilingual dictionaries se text translate karta hai. Ye koi training data use nahi karta. <Cite id="nlp-applications-wiki-rbmt" />

**Teen types** <Cite id="nlp-applications-gfg-mt" />

1. **Direct:** dictionary se har source word replace karo, phir kuch rules se words reorder karo. Simple hai par kachcha.
2. **Transfer:** source sentence ko analyse karke parse banao, transfer rules se use target structure mein badlo, phir target words generate karo.
3. **Interlingua:** sentence ko language-free meaning mein convert karo, phir usse target language generate karo.

**Example (English to Hindi):** "I eat apples" ka order subject, verb, object hai. Hindi mein subject, object, verb chahiye, to transfer rule use reorder karke *main seb khata hoon* ("I apples eat") bana deta hai.

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| Predictable, rules inspect kar sakte hain | Rules likhna slow aur costly hai |
| Bade parallel corpus ki zaroorat nahi | Saare exceptions aur idioms cover nahi hote |
| Related languages ke liye achha | Nayi language pairs pe scale karna mushkil |

**Conclusion:** RBMT linguistic rules aur dictionaries se translate karta hai; jo cases cover hain unme accurate hai, par general use ke liye statistical aur neural systems ne ise replace kar diya. <Cite id="nlp-applications-slp3-13" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "Rule-based MT uses linguistic rules and bilingual dictionaries; its types are direct, transfer and interlingua."
