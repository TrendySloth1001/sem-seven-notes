**In simple words:** insaan bachpan se language seekhte hain aur common sense bina koshish ke use karte hain. Computer ke paas sirf rules aur data hota hai, isliye jo cheezein humein aasaan lagti hain wo uske liye mushkil hoti hain. Yehi NLP ke challenges hain. <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-ibm-nlp" />

**Main challenges**

1. **Ambiguity.** Ek word ya sentence ke ek se zyada meaning ho sakte hain. Ye sabse bada challenge hai. <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-gfg-ambiguity" />
2. **Context aur world knowledge.** Sahi meaning situation pe depend karta hai. "It is cold in here" ka matlab asal mein "please window band karo" ho sakta hai.
3. **Figurative language.** Idioms, sarcasm aur humour word-by-word padhne pe fail ho jaate hain: "kick the bucket" ka bucket se kuch lena-dena nahi.
4. **Variation.** Slang, spelling mistakes, short forms ("gr8"), dialects, aur mixed languages jaise Hinglish.
5. **Reference.** *he*, *it*, *they* jaise pronouns ko sahi noun se jodna padta hai.
6. **Bahut saari languages aur kam data.** Rich morphology aur alag word order ki wajah se ek method doosri language pe fail ho jaata hai, aur kai languages ka training text bahut kam hai. <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-slp3" />
7. **Language badalti rehti hai.** Naye words aate rehte hain (*selfie*, *doomscroll*), isliye fixed dictionary purani ho jaati hai.

| Challenge | Example |
|---|---|
| Lexical ambiguity | *bank*: money bank ya river bank <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-wiki-polysemy" /> |
| Syntactic ambiguity | "I saw the man with a telescope": telescope kiske paas hai? |
| Idiom | "It is raining cats and dogs" |
| Reference | "Ravi told Amit that he won": kaun jeeta? |

**Conclusion:** NLP mushkil hai kyunki language ambiguous hai, context pe depend karti hai, badalti rehti hai, aur uske liye aisi world knowledge chahiye jo computer ke paas default mein nahi hoti.

Exam mein English mein likho: "The main challenges of NLP are ambiguity, context dependence, figurative language, variation, reference resolution and language diversity."
