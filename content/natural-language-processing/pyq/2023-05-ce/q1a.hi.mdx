**In simple words:** NLP ka main challenge ye hai ki natural language ambiguous hai, aur machine ko har stage par intended meaning dhundna padta hai.

| Challenge | Example |
|---|---|
| Lexical ambiguity: ek word, kai meanings | bank (financial ya river) |
| Syntactic ambiguity: ek sentence, kai structures | I saw a man with telescope |
| Semantic ambiguity: words ka logic | All students did not pass the exam |
| Anaphoric ambiguity: pronoun kaun sa noun hai | Mary told Susan that she had won |
| Pragmatic ambiguity: asli intention | Do you know the time? |

**Conclusion:** shuru ki ek galat choice (galat word sense ya parse) baad ke stages bigaad deti hai.
