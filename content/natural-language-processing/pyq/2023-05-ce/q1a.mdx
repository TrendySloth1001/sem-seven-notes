**In simple words:** the main challenge of NLP is that natural language is ambiguous, and the machine must find the intended meaning at every stage.

| Challenge | Example |
|---|---|
| Lexical ambiguity: one word, many meanings | bank (financial or river) |
| Syntactic ambiguity: one sentence, many structures | I saw a man with telescope |
| Semantic ambiguity: the logic of the words | All students did not pass the exam |
| Anaphoric ambiguity: which noun does the pronoun mean | Mary told Susan that she had won |
| Pragmatic ambiguity: the real intention | Do you know the time? |

Other challenges: figurative language such as idioms and metaphors ("I'm falling for you") <Cite id="class-nlp-asol" loc="p. 1" />, words the model has not seen (out-of-vocabulary words), and models that work in one domain but not in another <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 5" />.

**Conclusion:** one wrong choice early (a wrong word sense or parse) spoils the later stages.
