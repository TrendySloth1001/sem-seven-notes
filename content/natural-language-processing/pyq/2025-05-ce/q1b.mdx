**In simple words:** morphological analysis gives the root word plus tags that say its grammar. The output is the lemma (root) + morphological tags. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 9" />

| Word type | Input word | Morphological analysis (output) |
|---|---|---|
| Regular verb | walked | walk + V + Past |
| Irregular verb | went | go + V + Past |
| Singular noun | cat | cat + N + Sg |
| Plural noun | cats | cat + N + Pl |

- walked: walk is the root, V means verb, Past means past tense.
- went: the surface form is different, but the lemma is go, so irregular forms need lexical information (a dictionary).
- cat: noun, singular. cats: cat is the root, Pl means plural.

**Conclusion:** analysis = root + tags.
