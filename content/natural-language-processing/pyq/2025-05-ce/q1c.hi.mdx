**In simple words:** dono models words ko achhe se tag karte hain par dono ke kamzor points hain. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 5" /> <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 10" />

**HMM ki limitations** <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 5" />

1. **Strong independence assumption:** agla state sirf current state par depend karta hai, aur har word sirf apne tag par.
2. **Limited context:** ye long-range dependencies nahi pakad sakta.
3. **Data sparsity:** bada labelled corpus chahiye.
4. **Unseen words:** out-of-vocabulary words ko achhe se handle nahi karta.
5. **Generative model:** extra features (grammar, meaning) add karna mushkil.
6. Vocabulary ya states zyaada ho to **slow**.

**Maximum Entropy model ki limitations** <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq3" loc="p. 10" />

1. **Label bias problem:** probabilities har step par locally normalize hoti hain, poore sentence par nahi, isliye kam outgoing choices wale state se high-probability move decoding par haavi ho sakta hai, chahe poora sequence unlikely ho.
2. **Computational cost:** training mein iterative numerical optimization (L-BFGS, GIS, IIS) lagta hai, jo HMM ki simple counting se bhaari hai.

| Model | Main weakness |
|---|---|
| HMM | Independence assumptions aur rich features nahi |
| MaxEnt (MEMM) | Label bias aur costly training |

**Conclusion:** HMM apni assumptions se limited hai, MaxEnt label bias aur training cost se.
