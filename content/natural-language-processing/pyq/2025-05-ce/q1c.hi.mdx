**In simple words:** dono models sentence ke har word ko tag karte hain (noun, verb ...), aur dono ki kamzoriyan hain. <Cite id="hmm-maxent-crf-slp3-ch18" loc="§18.5" /> <Cite id="hmm-maxent-crf-gfg-hmm-ml" />

**Limitations of HMM:**

1. **Extra clues add karna mushkil.** Isme sirf do tables hain, tag-to-tag aur tag-to-word. "Capital letter se shuru" ya "-ed pe khatam" jaise clues fit nahi hote.
2. **Unknown words.** Jo word training mein kabhi nahi dikha, uski probability har tag ke liye 0 hoti hai, to path mar jaata hai jab tak guess na jodein.
3. **Strong independence assumptions.** Tag sirf pichle tag pe depend karta hai, aur word sirf apne tag pe.
4. **Zero counts.** Na dikhe tag pairs ke liye smoothing chahiye.
5. **Generative.** Ye words kaise bante hain wo model karta hai, seedha best tags nahi.
6. Bada tagged corpus chahiye.

**Limitations of MaxEnt:**

1. **Label bias.** Har step akele normalise hota hai, to kam choices wala tag word ko ignore kar deta hai. Baad ke words early galti theek nahi kar sakte. <Cite id="hmm-maxent-crf-wiki-memm" /> <Cite id="hmm-maxent-crf-lafferty2001" />
2. **Training mein sahi pichla tag** use hota hai, lekin test time pe wo sirf guess hai, to errors badhte jaate hain.
3. **Features haath se likhne padte hain**, aur zyada features se training slow hoti hai.
4. Generative nahi hai, to unlabelled text use nahi kar sakta.

| | HMM | MaxEnt |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Generative | Discriminative |
| Extra clues | Hard | Easy |
| Main weakness | Unknown words, independence | Label bias |

**Conclusion:** HMM simple hai par rigid; MaxEnt flexible hai par label bias se pareshan, jise CRF theek karta hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "HMM cannot use rich features and fails on unknown words; MaxEnt suffers from label bias."
