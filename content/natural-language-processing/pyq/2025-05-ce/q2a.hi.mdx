**In simple words:** **n-gram** matlab line se $n$ words ki ek run. **Bigram** ($n = 2$) matlab paas-paas ke do words ki jodi. Language model in runs ki counts se batata hai ki sentence kitna likely hai. <Cite id="ngram-language-models-slp3-ch3" /> <Cite id="ngram-language-models-gfg-ngram" /> <Cite id="ngram-language-models-wiki-ngram" />

**Kyun:** chain rule se $P(w_1 \dots w_k) = \prod_i P(w_i \mid w_1 \dots w_{i-1})$, lekin lambi history corpus mein kabhi nahi milti. **Markov assumption** sirf last $n-1$ words rakhta hai.

- **N-gram:** $P(w_i \mid w_{i-n+1} \dots w_{i-1}) = \dfrac{C(w_{i-n+1} \dots w_i)}{C(w_{i-n+1} \dots w_{i-1})}$
- **Bigram:** $P(w_i \mid w_{i-1}) = \dfrac{C(w_{i-1}\, w_i)}{C(w_{i-1})}$, aur $P(\text{sentence}) = \prod_i P(w_i \mid w_{i-1})$.

Yahan $C$ corpus mein count hai. Unigram, bigram, trigram matlab $n = 1, 2, 3$.

<Sum>

<Given>

Paanch sentences, har ek `<s>` se shuru aur `</s>` pe khatam: (1) Sam I am (2) Sam I like (3) I am Sam (4) Sam I do like (5) do I like Sam.

</Given>

<Find>

(a) se (d) ke liye likely next words, aur sentences (e), (f), (g) ki probabilities.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Counts.** `<s>`=5 (I 1, Sam 3, do 1). Sam=5 (I 3, `</s>` 2). I=5 (am 2, like 2, do 1). am=2 (Sam 1, `</s>` 1). like=3 (`</s>` 2, Sam 1). do=2 (like 1, I 1). *Reason: bigram probability = pair count / pehle word ki count.*
2. **(a)** `<s> Sam` ke baad model Sam dekhta hai: I = 3/5 sabse zyada. Next word: **I**.
3. **(b)** `do` ke baad: like = 1/2 aur I = 1/2, tie. Next word: **like ya I** (barabar likely).
4. **(c)** `Sam` ke baad: **I** (3/5), (a) jaisa. *Reason: bigram model sirf last word dekhta hai.*
5. **(d)** `like` ke baad: `</s>` = 2/3, Sam = 1/3. Next: **`</s>`** (sentence khatam).
6. **(e)** `<s> Sam I do I like </s>` $= \frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3} = \frac{36}{3750} = 0.0096$.
7. **(f)** `<s> Sam I am </s>` $= \frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{18}{250} = 0.072$.
8. **(g)** `<s> I do like Sam I am </s>` $= \frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{3}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{5}\cdot\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{7500} = 0.0008$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

(a) I, (b) like ya I (tie), (c) I, (d) `</s>`. Probabilities: (e) 0.0096, (f) 0.072, (g) 0.0008, to **(f) sabse better sentence hai**.

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Check 1: har history ki probabilities ka sum 1 hai: `<s>` $\frac15 + \frac35 + \frac15 = 1$, Sam $\frac35 + \frac25 = 1$, I $\frac25 + \frac25 + \frac15 = 1$.

Check 2: (e) decimals mein: $0.6 \times 0.6 = 0.36$, $\times 0.2 = 0.072$, $\times 0.5 = 0.036$, $\times 0.4 = 0.0144$, $\times 0.667 = 0.0096$, wahi answer.

Check 3: (f) chhota sentence hai aur common pairs se bana hai, (g) lamba hai aur rare pairs (`<s> I`, `I do`) hain, isliye order (f) > (e) > (g) sahi lagta hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Limit:** na dikhi hui pair ki probability 0 ho jaati hai, isliye smoothing chahiye.

**Conclusion:** bigram probability ek count ratio hai; sabse achha sentence wo hai jiska product sabse bada ho, yahan (f).

Exam mein English mein likho: "Bigram probability is the count of the word pair divided by the count of the first word; the sentence with the highest product is the best."
