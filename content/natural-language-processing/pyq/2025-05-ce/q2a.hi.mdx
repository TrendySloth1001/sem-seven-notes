**In simple words:** bigram model sirf pichhla word dekhta hai. Word pairs gintne se probabilities milti hain; agla word sabse probable wala hai, aur sentence tab behtar hai jab uski probabilities ka product bada ho.

**Concepts aur formula:** N-gram is a continuous sequence of n words in a text; bigram two words ki sequence hai (unigram: ek word; trigram: teen). <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 8" /> <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 1" /> Bigram probability $P(B \mid A) = \text{count}(AB)/\text{count}(A)$ hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 9" /> General n-gram mein agla word pichhle n - 1 words par depend karta hai.

<Sum>

<Given>

Corpus: `<s>` Sam I am `</s>`; `<s>` Sam I like `</s>`; `<s>` I am Sam `</s>`; `<s>` Sam I do like `</s>`; `<s>` do I like Sam `</s>`.

</Given>

<Find>

1. (a) `<s>` Sam, (b) `<s>` Sam I do, (c) `<s>` Sam I am Sam, (d) `<s>` do I like ke baad ka sabse probable agla word; 2. (e) `<s>` Sam I do I like `</s>`, (f) `<s>` Sam I am `</s>`, (g) `<s>` I do like Sam I am `</s>` mein se kiski probability sabse high hai.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Unigram counts:** `<s>` 5, Sam 5, I 5, am 2, like 3, do 2, `</s>` 5 (27 tokens, 22 bigrams).
2. **Bigram probabilities:**

Table mein n(AB) = count(AB), n(A) = count(A) aur P = P(B given A).

| Bigram AB | n(AB) | n(A) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<s>` Sam | 3 | 5 | 3/5 = 0.60 |
| Sam I | 3 | 5 | 3/5 = 0.60 |
| I am | 2 | 5 | 2/5 = 0.40 |
| am `</s>` | 1 | 2 | 1/2 = 0.50 |
| I like | 2 | 5 | 2/5 = 0.40 |
| like `</s>` | 2 | 3 | 2/3 = 0.67 |
| `<s>` I | 1 | 5 | 1/5 = 0.20 |
| am Sam | 1 | 2 | 1/2 = 0.50 |
| Sam `</s>` | 2 | 5 | 2/5 = 0.40 |
| I do | 1 | 5 | 1/5 = 0.20 |
| do like | 1 | 2 | 1/2 = 0.50 |
| `<s>` do | 1 | 5 | 1/5 = 0.20 |
| do I | 1 | 2 | 1/2 = 0.50 |
| like Sam | 1 | 3 | 1/3 = 0.33 |

3. **Agle words:** (a) Sam ke baad: I 3/5 = 0.60 jabki `</s>` 2/5, isliye **I**. (b) do ke baad: like 1/2 aur I 1/2 barabar hain, isliye **like ya I**. (c) Sam ke baad: phir **I** (0.60). (d) like ke baad: `</s>` 2/3 = 0.67 jabki Sam 1/3, isliye **`</s>`**.
4. **(e)** $P = 3/5 \times 3/5 \times 1/5 \times 1/2 \times 2/5 \times 2/3 = 6/625 = 0.0096$.
5. **(f)** $P = 3/5 \times 3/5 \times 2/5 \times 1/2 = 9/125 = 0.072$.
6. **(g)** $P = 1/5 \times 1/5 \times 1/2 \times 1/3 \times 3/5 \times 2/5 \times 1/2 = 1/1250 = 0.0008$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

Agle words: (a) I, (b) like ya I (tie), (c) I, (d) `</s>`. Sabse achha sentence **(f) `<s>` Sam I am `</s>`** hai, probability 0.072.

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Har next-word group ka sum 1 hai (Sam ke baad 3/5 + 2/5; do ke baad 1/2 + 1/2; like ke baad 2/3 + 1/3). (f) ko decimals mein multiply karo: 0.6 x 0.6 x 0.4 x 0.5 = 0.072. Ordering 0.072 > 0.0096 > 0.0008 dikhati hai ki rare pairs wale lambe sentences ka score kam aata hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Conclusion:** bigram model counts se agla word predict karta hai, aur sentences ko unki bigram probabilities ke product se rank karta hai.
