**In simple words:** morphology padhti hai ki words chhote parts (morphemes) se kaise bante hain. **Inflectional** morphology word ko sirf grammar ke hisaab se badalti hai, to word wahi rehta hai. **Derivational** morphology naya word banati hai jiska naya meaning hota hai, aur aksar word class bhi badal jaati hai. <Cite id="morphology-fst-wiki-inflection" /> <Cite id="morphology-fst-wiki-derivation" /> <Cite id="morphology-fst-gfg-morph" />

**Examples:** `cat` se `cats` (plural) aur `walk` se `walked` (past tense) inflection hain. `happy` se `happiness` (adjective se noun) aur `teach` se `teacher` (verb se noun) derivation hain.

| Point | Inflectional | Derivational |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Grammar dikhata hai: number, tense, person, case | Naya meaning wala naya word banata hai |
| Word class | Nahi badalti (noun noun hi rehta hai) | Aksar badalti hai (verb se noun) |
| Meaning | Core meaning wahi | Naya ya badla hua meaning |
| Affixes in English | Kam aur fixed: `-s`, `-ed`, `-ing`, `-er`, `-est` | Bahut: `un-`, `re-`, `-ness`, `-able`, `-ment` |
| Position | Sabse bahar, derivational suffix ke baad | Stem ke paas |
| How regular | Bahut regular, lagbhag har word pe lagta hai | Irregular, kuch words pe hi lagta hai |
| Dictionary | Saare forms ki ek entry (lemma) | Apni alag entry |
| Example | `play` se `plays`, `playing` | `play` se `player`, `playful` |

Word ke andar order: `nation` + `-al` + `-ize` + `-ed` mein pehle derivation (`nationalize`), phir inflection (`-ed`) sabse last mein aata hai. <Cite id="morphology-fst-jm-ch3-notes" />

**Use in NLP:** lemmatizer inflection hatakar lemma nikalta hai; stemmer dono tarah ke affix kaat deta hai.

**Conclusion:** inflection grammar ke liye word ka form badalti hai, derivation word ko hi badal deti hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Inflectional morphology changes the form of a word for grammar without changing its class; derivational morphology creates a new word, often of a different class."
