**In simple words:** perplexity batati hai ki language model ek text se kitna surprise hota hai. Perplexity jitni kam, model utna achha.

**Definition:** perplexity is a measurement of uncertainty in the predictions of a language model. It indicates how surprised a model is by the actual outcomes. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 5" />

$$
\text{Perplexity}(P) = 2^{-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\log_2 P(w_i \mid w_1, \dots, w_{i-1})}
$$

Ye words ki unke context ke diye jaane par average negative log-likelihood ka exponential hai. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 5" />

| Perplexity | Matlab |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ideal score: model har baar agla word perfectly predict karta hai |
| 1 se upar | Thodi uncertainty; jitni zyada, model utna kam confident |
| 10 | Model utna uncertain hai jaise 10 alag possible agle words mein se chun raha ho |

Aakhri row ka check: agar har word ki probability 1/10 ho, to perplexity $(10^{-N})^{-1/N} = 10$.

**Conclusion:** language models ko compare karne ke liye perplexity use karo; jiski value kam ho wo behtar hai.
