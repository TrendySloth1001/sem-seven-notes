**In simple words:** **bigram** neighbouring words ka jodi hai. Pehle word ke baad doosre word ki probability counting se milti hai: $P(w_i \mid w_{i-1}) = \dfrac{C(w_{i-1}\,w_i)}{C(w_{i-1})}$, jahan $C$ corpus mein count hai. `<s>` aur `</s>` sentence ki start aur end dikhate hain. Words jaise likhe hain waise hi rakhe hain (*Sleep* aur *sleep* yahan alag hain). <Cite id="ngram-language-models-slp3-ch3" /> <Cite id="ngram-language-models-gfg-ngram" />

<Sum>

<Given>

Corpus: `<s> I tell you to sleep and rest </s>`, `<s> I would like to sleep for an hour </s>`, `<s> Sleep helps one to relax </s>`.

</Given>

<Find>

saare bigrams, unki conditional probabilities, aur *to* ke baad sabse likely next word.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Bigrams list karo** sentence by sentence: sentence 1 mein 8, sentence 2 mein 9, sentence 3 mein 6, to $8 + 9 + 6 = 23$ bigram tokens. Do repeat hote hain (`<s> I` aur `to sleep` do baar aate hain), isliye 21 alag bigrams hain.
2. **Har first word ka count** $C(w_{i-1})$: `<s>` 3, I 2, tell 1, you 1, to 3, sleep 2, and 1, rest 1, would 1, like 1, for 1, an 1, hour 1, Sleep 1, helps 1, one 1, relax 1.
3. **Divide karo** bigram count ko first-word count se:

   | Bigram | Count | Probability |
   |---|---|---|
   | `<s>` I | 2 | 2/3 |
   | `<s>` Sleep | 1 | 1/3 |
   | I tell | 1 | 1/2 |
   | I would | 1 | 1/2 |
   | tell you | 1 | 1 |
   | you to | 1 | 1 |
   | to sleep | 2 | 2/3 |
   | to relax | 1 | 1/3 |
   | sleep and | 1 | 1/2 |
   | sleep for | 1 | 1/2 |
   | and rest | 1 | 1 |
   | rest `</s>` | 1 | 1 |
   | would like | 1 | 1 |
   | like to | 1 | 1 |
   | for an | 1 | 1 |
   | an hour | 1 | 1 |
   | hour `</s>` | 1 | 1 |
   | Sleep helps | 1 | 1 |
   | helps one | 1 | 1 |
   | one to | 1 | 1 |
   | relax `</s>` | 1 | 1 |

4. ***to* ke baad next word.** *to* 3 baar aata hai, uske baad *sleep* (do baar) aur *relax* (ek baar). $P(\text{sleep} \mid \text{to}) = 2/3 \approx 0.67$ aur $P(\text{relax} \mid \text{to}) = 1/3 \approx 0.33$. *Reason: jis word ki conditional probability sabse high ho wahi predict hota hai.*

</Steps>

<Answer>

*to* ke baad next word **sleep** hai, probability 2/3 (relax ki 1/3).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Ek word ke baad aane wale saare words ki probabilities ka sum 1 hona chahiye: `<s>` ke baad $2/3 + 1/3 = 1$; I ke baad $1/2 + 1/2 = 1$; *to* ke baad $2/3 + 1/3 = 1$; *sleep* ke baad $1/2 + 1/2 = 1$. Saath hi 21 alag bigrams ke counts ka sum 23 hai: $19 \times 1 + 2 \times 2 = 23$.

</Box>

</Sum>

Agar sab lower-case kar do, to *Sleep* aur *sleep* merge ho jaate hain; *to* ka answer nahi badalta.
