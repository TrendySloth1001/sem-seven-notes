**In simple words:** a **bigram** is a pair of neighbouring words. The probability of the second word given the first is found by counting: $P(w_i \mid w_{i-1}) = \dfrac{C(w_{i-1}\,w_i)}{C(w_{i-1})}$, where $C$ is a count in the corpus. `<s>` and `</s>` mark the start and end of a sentence. Words are kept exactly as written (*Sleep* and *sleep* are different here). <Cite id="ngram-language-models-slp3-ch3" /> <Cite id="ngram-language-models-gfg-ngram" />

<Sum>

<Given>

Corpus: `<s> I tell you to sleep and rest </s>`, `<s> I would like to sleep for an hour </s>`, `<s> Sleep helps one to relax </s>`.

</Given>

<Find>

all bigrams, their conditional probabilities, and the most likely next word after *to*.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **List the bigrams** sentence by sentence: sentence 1 has 8, sentence 2 has 9, sentence 3 has 6, so $8 + 9 + 6 = 23$ bigram tokens. Two repeat (`<s> I` and `to sleep` appear twice), so there are 21 different bigrams.
2. **Count each first word** $C(w_{i-1})$: `<s>` 3, I 2, tell 1, you 1, to 3, sleep 2, and 1, rest 1, would 1, like 1, for 1, an 1, hour 1, Sleep 1, helps 1, one 1, relax 1.
3. **Divide** bigram count by first-word count:

   | Bigram | Count | Probability |
   |---|---|---|
   | `<s>` I | 2 | 2/3 |
   | `<s>` Sleep | 1 | 1/3 |
   | I tell | 1 | 1/2 |
   | I would | 1 | 1/2 |
   | tell you | 1 | 1 |
   | you to | 1 | 1 |
   | to sleep | 2 | 2/3 |
   | to relax | 1 | 1/3 |
   | sleep and | 1 | 1/2 |
   | sleep for | 1 | 1/2 |
   | and rest | 1 | 1 |
   | rest `</s>` | 1 | 1 |
   | would like | 1 | 1 |
   | like to | 1 | 1 |
   | for an | 1 | 1 |
   | an hour | 1 | 1 |
   | hour `</s>` | 1 | 1 |
   | Sleep helps | 1 | 1 |
   | helps one | 1 | 1 |
   | one to | 1 | 1 |
   | relax `</s>` | 1 | 1 |

4. **Next word after *to*.** *to* occurs 3 times, followed by *sleep* (twice) and *relax* (once). $P(\text{sleep} \mid \text{to}) = 2/3 \approx 0.67$ and $P(\text{relax} \mid \text{to}) = 1/3 \approx 0.33$. *Reason: the word with the highest conditional probability is predicted.*

</Steps>

<Answer>

the next word after *to* is **sleep**, with probability 2/3 (relax has 1/3).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Probabilities of all words after one word must add to 1: after `<s>`, $2/3 + 1/3 = 1$; after I, $1/2 + 1/2 = 1$; after *to*, $2/3 + 1/3 = 1$; after *sleep*, $1/2 + 1/2 = 1$. Also the counts of the 21 distinct bigrams add up to 23: $19 \times 1 + 2 \times 2 = 23$.

</Box>

</Sum>

If everything is lower-cased, *Sleep* and *sleep* merge; the answer for *to* does not change.
