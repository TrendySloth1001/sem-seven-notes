**In simple words:** ye chaar naam batate hain ki alag words (ya ek hi word) ke meanings aapas mein kaise related hain. <Cite id="lexical-semantics-slp3-ch5" />

**1. Homonymy.** Do ya zyada **unrelated** meanings ki spelling aur sound same hoti hai, lekin ye asal mein alag words hain jo ittefaq se ek jaise dikhte hain. *Bank* (nadi ka kinara) aur *bank* (paise ki institution) homonyms hain; waise hi *bat* (janwar) aur *bat* (cricket). Dictionary mein inki alag entries hoti hain. <Cite id="lexical-semantics-wiki-homonym" />

**2. Polysemy.** Ek word ke kai **related** senses. Insaan ka *mouth* aur nadi ka *mouth* dono mein opening ka idea hai; body ka *head* aur school ka *head* dono mein top ya leader ka idea hai. Dictionary inhe ek hi entry ke neeche rakhti hai. <Cite id="ambiguity-challenges-wiki-polysemy" />

**3. Synonymy.** Alag words jinka (lagbhag) **same meaning** hota hai: *big* aur *large*, *couch* aur *sofa*, *buy* aur *purchase*. Perfect synonyms rare hain; words aksar style ya use mein alag hote hain (*water* aur *H2O*). WordNet synonyms ko synset mein group karta hai. <Cite id="lexical-semantics-wiki-synonym" /> <Cite id="lexical-semantics-gfg-synonyms" />

**4. Antonymy.** **Opposite** meaning wale words. Types: gradable (*hot* aur *cold*, beech ki values ke saath), complementary (*alive* aur *dead*, beech ka kuch nahi) aur relational ya reverse (*buy* aur *sell*, *up* aur *down*). <Cite id="lexical-semantics-wiki-antonym" />

| Relation | Words | Meanings | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homonymy | Same form | Unrelated | *bank* (river) / *bank* (money) |
| Polysemy | Same word | Related | *mouth* of river / person |
| Synonymy | Different words | Same | *big* / *large* |
| Antonymy | Different words | Opposite | *hot* / *cold* |

**Tip:** agar do senses ke beech link dikhe to polysemy hai; agar kuch common nahi to homonymy.
