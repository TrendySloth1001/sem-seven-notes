**In simple words:** a word class (part of speech) groups words that behave alike in a sentence, such as nouns or verbs. Knowing the class of each word is the first step of tagging and parsing. <Cite id="pos-tagging-wiki-pos" /> <Cite id="pos-tagging-gfg-pos" />

Word classes fall into two families. **Open classes** keep getting new words (*selfie*, *google*). **Closed classes** are small, fixed sets of function words that rarely get new members. <Cite id="pos-tagging-slp3-ch18" />

**Open classes**

| Class | Job | Example | Penn tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noun | Names a person, place, thing or idea | *Mumbai, teacher, honesty* | NN, NNS, NNP |
| Verb | Shows action or state | *run, is, wrote* | VB, VBD, VBG, VBZ |
| Adjective | Describes a noun | *big, red, happy* | JJ |
| Adverb | Describes a verb, adjective or adverb | *quickly, very, here* | RB |

**Closed classes**

| Class | Job | Example | Penn tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pronoun | Stands in for a noun | *he, they, it* | PRP |
| Determiner | Marks a noun | *the, a, this* | DT |
| Preposition | Links a noun to the rest, shows place or time | *in, on, of, with* | IN |
| Conjunction | Joins words or clauses | *and, but, because* | CC, IN |
| Auxiliary and modal | Helps the main verb | *will, can* (modals), *has, is* (auxiliaries) | MD, VBZ |
| Particle | Small word joined to a verb | *up* in *give up* | RP |
| Numeral | Counts | *one, two, third* | CD |

Interjections (*oh, wow*) are often listed too.

**Example:** *The/DT quick/JJ fox/NN jumped/VBD quickly/RB.*

**Conclusion:** English has four open classes (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) and several closed function-word classes; a tagger must also decide the class when a word fits two (*book* as noun or verb).
