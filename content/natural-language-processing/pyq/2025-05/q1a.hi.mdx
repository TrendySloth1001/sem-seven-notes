**In simple words:** affix ek chhota piece hai jo word ko badalne ke liye jodte hain.

**Definition:** an affix is a bound morpheme added to the root or stem of a word to alter its meaning, create a new word, or change its grammatical function. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 3" />

**Position ke hisaab se types** <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 3" /> <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 4" />

| Type | Kahan judta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix | Root word ki shuruaat mein | unhappy mein un- meaning ko opposite bana deta hai |
| Suffix | Root word ke end mein | walked mein -ed, happiness mein -ness |
| Infix | Root word ke andar directly daala jaata hai | English mein rare ya nahi hota, doosri languages mein milta hai |

**Conclusion:** word = stem (root word) + affixes; success + un- + -ful se unsuccessful banta hai.
