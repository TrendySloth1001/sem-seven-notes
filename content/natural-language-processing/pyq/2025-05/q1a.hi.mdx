**In simple words:** affix ek chhota piece hota hai jo word mein jodke uska meaning ya grammar badal dete hain. Main word ko **stem** (root) kehte hain, jaise `play`. `un-`, `-ing` aur `-er` affixes hain. Affix akela word nahi ban sakta, isliye ye **bound morpheme** hai. <Cite id="morphology-fst-wiki-affix" /> <Cite id="morphology-fst-gfg-morph" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai:** NLP programs words ko stem aur affixes mein todte hain (morphological analysis), taaki `played`, `playing` aur `player` sab `play` se link ho jaayein.

**Affixes ke types:**

| Type | Kahan lagta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix | Stem se pehle | `un-happy`, `re-write`, `dis-agree` <Cite id="morphology-fst-wiki-prefix" /> |
| Suffix | Stem ke baad | `play-ing`, `kind-ness`, `walk-ed` <Cite id="morphology-fst-wiki-suffix" /> |
| Infix | Stem ke andar | Tagalog `sulat` (likhna) se `s-um-ulat` (likha). English mein sirf mazaak wale cases hain, jaise `abso-bloody-lutely` |
| Circumfix | Stem ke dono taraf | German `lieb` (pyaar) se `ge-lieb-t` (pyaar kiya) |

English mein mostly prefix aur suffix use hote hain. Kai dusri languages mein infix aur circumfix bhi hote hain.

**Kaam ke hisaab se:** affix *inflectional* ho sakta hai (sirf grammar, word class same: `cat` se `cats`) ya *derivational* (naya word banata hai, aksar nayi class: `happy` se `happiness`).

**Conclusion:** affixes stem se lage bound parts hain jo prefix, suffix, infix ya circumfix hote hain; stemmers aur morphological analysers inhe dhoondhkar hatate hain.

Exam mein English mein likho: "An affix is a bound morpheme attached to a stem; its types are prefix, suffix, infix and circumfix."
