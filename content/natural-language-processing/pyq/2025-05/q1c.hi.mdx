**In simple words:** log baar baar naam nahi dohrate. Wo *he*, *it*, *the girl* bolte hain. **Reference resolution** ka matlab hai ye pata lagana ki har aisa phrase kis asli cheez (entity) ko point kar raha hai. <Cite id="discourse-reference-slp3-coref" />

**Key terms:** **referring expression** wo phrase hai jo kisi cheez ko point karta hai (*she*, *the girl*, *Priya*). **Referent** wo cheez hai jisko point kiya. *Priya lost her bag* mein *her* **anaphor** hai aur *Priya* uska **antecedent**. Do phrases agar ek hi entity ko point karein to wo **corefer** karte hain. <Cite id="discourse-reference-wiki-anaphora" /> <Cite id="discourse-reference-wiki-coref" />

**Referring expressions ke kinds:** pronouns (*she*), definite noun phrases (*the girl*), indefinite noun phrases (*a girl*, jo nayi entity laata hai), proper names (*Priya*), demonstratives (*this book*).

**Ye problem kyun hai:** *Priya met Anita. She gave her a book.* mein dono ladkiyan *she* aur *her* ho sakti hain. *It is raining* mein *it* kisi ko point nahi karta. Kabhi kabhi sirf world knowledge madad karta hai: *The trophy did not fit in the suitcase because it was too big* (yahan *it* trophy hai).

**Problem kaise solve hota hai:**

1. Pehle ke text se candidate antecedents ikatthe karo.
2. **Constraints** se impossible candidates hatao: number, gender, person mein agreement; syntactic rules (*Jill likes him* mein *him* Jill nahi hai); verb ka meaning (*ate it* mein *it* khaane ki cheez hai).
3. Bache hue ko **preferences** se rank karo: recent mention, object ke upar subject, parallel sentences mein same role.
4. Best chuno. Methods: Hobbs' tree-walk, Centering, ya machine learning. <Cite id="anaphora-resolution-tp-nlp" />

**Conclusion:** reference resolution har pronoun aur noun phrase ko uski entity se jodta hai, pehle constraints aur phir preferences se; ye translation, summarisation aur question answering ke liye zaroori hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Reference resolution is the task of finding the entity that a referring expression points to."
