**In simple words:** word class (part of speech) un words ka group hai jo sentence mein ek jaisa behave karte hain, jaise nouns ya verbs. Har word ki class pata hona tagging aur parsing ka pehla step hai. <Cite id="pos-tagging-wiki-pos" /> <Cite id="pos-tagging-gfg-pos" />

Word classes do families mein aati hain. **Open classes** mein naye words judte rehte hain (*selfie*, *google*). **Closed classes** chhote, fixed function words ke sets hain jinme naye members kam hi aate hain. <Cite id="pos-tagging-slp3-ch18" />

**Open classes**

| Class | Kaam | Example | Penn tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noun | Person, place, thing ya idea ka naam | *Mumbai, teacher, honesty* | NN, NNS, NNP |
| Verb | Action ya state dikhata hai | *run, is, wrote* | VB, VBD, VBG, VBZ |
| Adjective | Noun ko describe karta hai | *big, red, happy* | JJ |
| Adverb | Verb, adjective ya adverb ko describe karta hai | *quickly, very, here* | RB |

**Closed classes**

| Class | Kaam | Example | Penn tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pronoun | Noun ki jagah aata hai | *he, they, it* | PRP |
| Determiner | Noun ko mark karta hai | *the, a, this* | DT |
| Preposition | Noun ko baaki sentence se jodta hai, place ya time dikhata hai | *in, on, of, with* | IN |
| Conjunction | Words ya clauses ko jodta hai | *and, but, because* | CC, IN |
| Auxiliary and modal | Main verb ki madad karta hai | *will, can* (modals), *has, is* (auxiliaries) | MD, VBZ |
| Particle | Verb ke saath juda chhota word | *give up* mein *up* | RP |
| Numeral | Ginti batata hai | *one, two, third* | CD |

Interjections (*oh, wow*) bhi aksar list kiye jaate hain.

**Example:** *The/DT quick/JJ fox/NN jumped/VBD quickly/RB.*

**Conclusion:** English mein chaar open classes hain (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) aur kai closed function-word classes; jab ek word do class mein fit ho (*book* noun ya verb) to tagger ko class decide karni padti hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Word classes are divided into open classes (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) and closed classes (pronoun, determiner, preposition, conjunction, auxiliary, particle, numeral)."
