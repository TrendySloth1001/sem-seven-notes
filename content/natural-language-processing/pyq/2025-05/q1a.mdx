**In simple words:** an affix is a small piece added to a word to change it.

**Definition:** an affix is a bound morpheme added to the root or stem of a word to alter its meaning, create a new word, or change its grammatical function. <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 3" />

**Types by position** <Cite id="class-nlp-pyq2" loc="p. 3" /> <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 4" />

| Type | Where it attaches | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix | At the beginning of a root word | un- in unhappy changes the meaning to its opposite |
| Suffix | At the end of a root word | -ed in walked, -ness in happiness |
| Infix | Inserted directly inside a root word | Rare or non-existent in English, found in other languages |

**Conclusion:** a word is a stem (root word) plus affixes; success + un- + -ful gives unsuccessful.
