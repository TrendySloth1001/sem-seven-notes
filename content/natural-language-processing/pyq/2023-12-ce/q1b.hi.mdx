**In simple words:** language model ko jo word ya n-gram training mein nahi dikha, use probability zero mil jaati hai, aur isse poora sentence kharab ho jata hai. **Good-Turing discounting** ye theek karta hai: ye **ek baar** dikhi cheezon se andaza lagata hai ki **unseen** cheezon ko kitni probability milni chahiye, aur uske liye seen cheezon ke counts thode kam kar deta hai. <Cite id="ngram-language-models-wiki-gt" /> <Cite id="ngram-language-models-slp3-ch3" />

$N_c$ matlab un types ki sankhya jo theek $c$ baar dikhe, aur $N$ total tokens.

$$
P_{GT}(\text{unseen}) = \frac{N_1}{N}, \qquad c^{*} = (c+1)\,\frac{N_{c+1}}{N_c}, \qquad P_{GT}(x) = \frac{c^{*}}{N}
$$

<Sum>

<Given>

jheel se 18 machhliyan pakdi: carp 10, perch 3, whitefish 2, trout 1, salmon 1, eel 1. To $N = 18$, $N_1 = 3$, $N_2 = 1$.

</Given>

<Find>

agli machhli naye kism ki hone ki probability, aur trout ki Good-Turing probability.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Unseen mass.** $P(\text{new kind}) = N_1 / N = 3/18 = 1/6$. *Reason: 18 mein se teen baar sirf ek-ek baar dikhi.*
2. **Trout ka naya count.** $c^{*} = (1+1)\,N_2 / N_1 = 2 \times 1/3 = 2/3$. *Reason: formula dekhta hai kitni kismen do baar dikhi aur kitni ek baar.*
3. **Probability.** $P_{GT}(\text{trout}) = (2/3)/18 = 1/27 \approx 0.037$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

naya kism: $1/6 \approx 0.167$; trout: $1/27 \approx 0.037$ (seedhe $1/18 \approx 0.056$ se kam).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Step 3 dusre tareeke se: $(2/3)/18 = 2/54 = 1/27$. Trout $1/18$ se $1/27$ ho gaya, yani kuch probability sach mein unseen kismon ko chali gayi, jaisa hona chahiye.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Limit:** jab $N_{c+1} = 0$ ho to formula fail hota hai (yahan $N_4 = 0$, isliye perch adjust nahi ho sakta); practice mein pehle $N_c$ values ko smooth karte hain. <Cite id="ngram-language-models-gale-sampson1995" />

**Conclusion:** Good-Turing frequency-of-frequencies se counts dobara estimate karta hai aur unseen events ko fair hissa, $N_1/N$, deta hai.
