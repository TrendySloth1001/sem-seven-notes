**In simple words:** tokenization text ko words mein kaatta hai; stemming words ke endings kaat deta hai.

| Point | Tokenization | Stemming |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | Text ke continuous stream ko chhote, meaningful units yaani tokens (usually words) mein todna <Cite id="class-nlp-asol" loc="p. 11" /> | Morphological analysis ka simplified form jo root word dhundne ke liye use hota hai <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 19" /> |
| Kispar kaam | Poore text par | Har word par |
| Example | "NLP is fun" se "NLP", "is", "fun" milta hai <Cite id="class-nlp-m2" loc="p. 18" /> | retrieval, retrieved, retrieving se retriev milta hai <Cite id="class-nlp-asol" loc="p. 12" /> |
| Note | Hyphenated words aur bina spaces wali languages mein mushkil | Crude: stem dictionary word na ho sakta hai |

**Conclusion:** pehle tokenize karo, phir har token ko stem karo.
