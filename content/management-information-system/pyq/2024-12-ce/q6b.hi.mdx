**In simple words:** e-commerce matlab internet pe goods aur services kharidna aur bechna. Aap Amazon ya Flipkart kholte ho, product chunte ho, UPI se pay karte ho aur wo ghar pe aa jaata hai, shop gaye bina. <Cite id="e-commerce-wiki-ecommerce" />

**Definition:** e-commerce computer networks, mainly internet, use karke products, services ya information ka buying, selling aur exchange hai. Ye e-business ka part hai, jismein supply chain chalana aur customers ko serve karna jaise internal kaam bhi aate hain. <Cite id="e-commerce-rainer-intro-is" />

**Ye kyun badha:** wide reach, 24 ghante service, kam cost, zyada choice, easy price comparison, UPI payments, aur sasta smartphone aur data.

**Types (models)**

| Type | Between | Example |
|---|---|---|
| B2B | Business to business | Dukaan wholesaler se IndiaMART ya Alibaba pe stock kharidti hai |
| B2C | Business to consumer | Amazon, Flipkart, Zomato |
| C2C | Consumer to consumer | OLX, eBay |
| C2B | Consumer to business | Freelancers firms ko services bechte hain |
| B2G | Business to government | Firms government tenders ke liye bid karti hain |

**Advantages aur disadvantages**

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| 24 ghante khula, internet wali kisi bhi jagah tak pahunch | Product chhoo ya try nahi kar sakte, isliye returns common |
| Badi choice, easy price comparison | Fraud, stolen cards, fake sites |
| Sellers ke liye kam cost | Delivery delay, costly last mile |
| Customer data se personal offers | Privacy concerns |

**Applications:** shopping (Amazon, Flipkart), food delivery (Zomato), travel (IRCTC), banking (SBI YONO), tickets (BookMyShow). <Cite id="e-commerce-gfg-intro" /> <Cite id="e-commerce-ibm" />

**Conclusion:** e-commerce badalta hai ki log kaise kharidte hain aur firms kaise bechti hain, aur ye tabhi chalta hai jab information system, payments aur delivery teeno reliable hon.
