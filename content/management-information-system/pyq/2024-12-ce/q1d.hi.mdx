**In simple words:** company ka social media account ab wo complaint desk hai jo poori duniya ko dikhta hai. Tez aur polite public reply baaki sab customers ko batata hai ki firm care karti hai. Social computing matlab web tools, jaise social networks, chat apps aur forums, use karna taaki log interact aur share kar sakein. <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-social-computing" />

**Social computing customer service ko kaise inspire kar raha hai**

1. **Fast, public support:** customer problem post karta hai aur firm reply karti hai. Indian Railways ke passengers train aur coach ke issues X pe raise karte hain aur railway service accounts jawab dete hain.
2. **24 x 7 help chatbots se:** WhatsApp aur websites pe bots order status jaise simple sawalon ke jawab dete hain. SBI WhatsApp banking deta hai balance aur mini-statement ke liye. <Cite id="crm-wiki-chatbot" />
3. **Customer communities aur forums:** doosre customers sawalon ke jawab dete hain, isse support team pe load kam hota hai.
4. **Social listening:** firm apne brand name ko search karti hai aur complaints dhoondh leti hai, chahe kisi ne tag na kiya ho.
5. **Self-help content:** FAQs, how-to videos aur wikis se customers khud problems solve kar lete hain.
6. **Feedback loop:** reviews aur complaints dikhate hain ki product mein kya theek karna hai.
7. **Social CRM:** staff ko pehle ke posts dikhte hain aur wo friendly tareeke se jawab dete hain, aur complaint CRM system mein service ticket ban jaati hai. <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-social-crm" />

| Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|
| Quick, low-cost support | Kharab jawab sabko dikhta hai |
| Problems jaldi pakadti hain | Customers instant reply expect karte hain |
| Trust aur loyalty badhti hai | Messages ka bada volume sambhalna mushkil |

**Example:** Zomato aur Amazon social media aur chat pe complaints ka jawab dete hain aur feedback se service improve karte hain. <Cite id="crm-gfg-intro" />

**Conclusion:** social computing customer service ko open, two-way conversation bana deta hai, jo loyalty badhata hai agar firm tez aur achha reply de.
