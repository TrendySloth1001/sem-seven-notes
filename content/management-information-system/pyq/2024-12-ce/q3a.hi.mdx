**In simple words:** cyber security matlab computers, networks aur data ko attack, damage ya theft se bachana. Ye koi ek tool nahi hai. Ye technology, people aur process ka milakar kaam hai, jo baar-baar dohraya jaata hai kyunki attackers apne tareeke badalte rehte hain. <Cite id="note-information-security-nist-csf" /> <Cite id="note-information-security-ibm-infosec" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai:** malware, phishing, ransomware aur denial of service jaise threats business rok sakte hain, customer data leak kar sakte hain aur legal trouble la sakte hain. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cyber-attacks" />

```mermaid
%% caption: Defence in depth. Ek layer fail ho jaaye to agli layer centre ke data ko bachati hai.

  A["People: training and awareness"] --> B["Policies and audits"]
  B --> C["Network: firewall, IDS, VPN"]

  E --> F["Data: encryption and backups"]
```

**Measures examples ke saath**

| # | Measure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strong passwords aur multi-factor authentication (MFA) | Banking apps password ke saath OTP ya fingerprint maangte hain |
| 2 | Software updates (patching) | 2017 ka Windows patch WannaCry ko rok sakta tha |
| 3 | Antivirus aur anti-malware | Endpoint protection download mein chhupa trojan block karta hai |
| 4 | Firewall aur network security | Company firewall known bad addresses ka traffic rokta hai |
| 5 | Encryption | Flipkart checkout pe HTTPS; encrypted laptop disks |
| 6 | Regular, tested backups | Firm ransomware ke baad bina paise diye files restore karti hai |
| 7 | Access control aur least privilege | Clerk payroll database nahi khol sakta |
| 8 | Training aur awareness | Bank staff ke liye mock phishing drills chalata hai |
| 9 | Security policy aur audits | Annual audit attackers se pehle open server pakad leta hai |
| 10 | Monitoring aur incident response plan | Security team infected laptop ko minutes mein isolate karti hai |
| 11 | Reporting aur legal compliance | Fraud ko cyber crime helpline aur CERT-In ko report karna |

<Cite id="note-information-security-cisa-practices" />

**Advantages:** kam breaches, customer trust aur legal compliance. **Limits:** cost, user ko inconvenience, aur koi bhi measure 100 percent safety nahi deta, isiliye layers use hoti hain.

**Conclusion:** achhi cyber security strong technology, trained people aur clear rules ko milati hai, aur use lagatar review karna padta hai.
