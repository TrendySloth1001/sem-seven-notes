**In simple words:** jab koi firm computers, networks aur software laati hai, to sirf tools nahi badalte. Firm ka structure, uske jobs, managers ke decide karne ka tareeka aur workers ki health, sab badalta hai. Socho ek school paper register chhod ke attendance app use karta hai: principal ko absent students turant dikh jaate hain, aur jo clerk monthly reports banata tha uska kaam ab alag hai. <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" loc="Ch. 1" />

**Definition:** IT ka organizations pe impact matlab wo changes jo IT laata hai firm ke structure mein, managers aur employees ke kaam karne mein, aur workplace ki safety aur health mein.

**Main impacts**

1. **Flatter structure:** systems reports collect aur summarize kar dete hain jo pehle middle managers upar bhejte the, isliye layers kam hoti hain aur ek manager zyada logon ko supervise karta hai. Practice mein effect mixed hai, isliye likho "IT tends to flatten organizations".
2. **Manager ka job badalta hai:** managers ko fast, detailed information milti hai, wo jaldi decide karte hain aur email aur video se remote teams lead karte hain.
3. **Jobs jaati bhi hain, aati bhi hain:** data entry jaisa routine kaam automate ho jaata hai, aur developers, data analysts aur security staff ke naye jobs aate hain.
4. **Health aur comfort:** screen pe lambe ghante bitane se eye strain, back pain aur repetitive strain injury hoti hai. Achhi ergonomics (body ke hisaab se chairs, screens aur keyboards) isse kam karti hai. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-ergonomics" />
5. **Stress aur monitoring:** hamesha available rehna aur track hona stress badhata hai.

| Area | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Flatter, faster communication | Middle-manager posts kam |
| Decisions | Real-time information | Information overload |
| Jobs | Naye skilled jobs | Routine jobs automate |
| Health | Bhaari manual kaam kam | Eye strain, back pain, stress |

**Example:** SBI apni hazaaron branches ek hi core-banking system pe chalata hai, isliye head office ko branch ke figures jaldi dikh jaate hain, manual reports ki kai layers ke bina.

**Conclusion:** IT organizations ko faster aur flatter banata hai, lekin managers ko benefits ke saath retraining, ergonomics aur stress ka bhi plan banana padta hai.
