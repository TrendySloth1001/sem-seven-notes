**In simple words:** a computer network connects computers so they can share data and resources. Almost everything we do online runs on one.

**Definition:** a computer network is a set of computers and devices connected by cables or wireless links so they can exchange data and share resources such as files, printers and an Internet connection. <Cite id="networks-wireless-rainer-iis" /> <Cite id="networks-wireless-wiki-computer-network" />

**Applications**

1. **Resource sharing:** many users share one printer, storage drive or software licence. Example: a college lab with one printer.
2. **Information sharing:** files and databases sit on a server, so everyone sees the same latest copy. Example: a bank branch reads a balance from the central database.
3. **Communication:** email, chat, voice and video calls. Example: Infosys staff in Pune and London meet on video.
4. **E-commerce and online services:** shopping, payments and order tracking. Example: Amazon, Flipkart, UPI.
5. **Cloud computing and remote work:** staff reach office systems from home. Example: Zomato's app talks to cloud servers.
6. **Banking and finance:** ATMs, net banking and cards connect to the bank's central system. Example: SBI's ATM network.
7. **Education and entertainment:** online classes, streaming, games. Example: NPTEL, Netflix.
8. **Backup and control:** data is copied to another machine, and an administrator sets rules for all users from one place. <Cite id="networks-wireless-gfg-importance" /> <Cite id="networks-wireless-ibm-networking" />

**Disadvantages:** set-up cost, one failure can stop many users, and a virus can spread quickly.

**Conclusion:** networks let people and systems share resources and information, which is why every modern business depends on them.
