**In simple words:** information systems (IS) sirf companies ki madad nahi karte. Wo aam logon ke jeene, seekhne, health care lene aur government se deal karne ka tareeka bhi badal dete hain. <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" loc="Ch. 1" />

**Society ke liye IS ki importance**

1. **Quality of life:** online shopping, banking, booking aur government services time aur travel bachate hain. Work from home flexibility deta hai.
2. **Robots aur automation:** machines khatarnak, ganda ya repetitive kaam karti hain, jaise car factories mein welding aur warehouses mein items pick karna.
3. **Health care:** electronic health records, medical imaging aur online consultations (telehealth) se doctors jaldi decide karte hain aur door ke patients tak pahunch paate hain. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-telehealth" />
4. **Education:** NPTEL aur SWAYAM jaise online courses internet wale kisi bhi insaan tak pahunchte hain.
5. **Information aur awaaz tak access:** search engines aur social media se koi bhi facts dhoond sakta hai aur apni baat rakh sakta hai. <Cite id="it-impact-society-gfg-media-society" />
6. **Government services:** online tax filing aur UPI payments queues aur paperwork kam karte hain.
7. **Disabled logon ke liye:** assistive technology unhe padhne, kaam karne aur communicate karne mein madad karti hai.

**IS jo problems banata hai**

- Job loss aur nayi skills seekhne ki zaroorat.
- Privacy aur security risks.
- **Digital divide**: jinke paas devices, internet ya skills nahi hain wo peeche reh jaate hain. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-digital-divide" />
- Fake news, screen addiction aur electronic waste.

**Conclusion:** IS ek shehar ki bijli jaisa hai. Ye zyada tar logon ki zindagi behtar karta hai, lekin society ko safety rules aur sabke liye access bhi chahiye.
