**In simple words:** computer network computers ko jodta hai taaki wo data aur resources share kar sakein. Online jo kuch bhi hum karte hain, wo sab kisi network pe chalta hai.

**Definition:** computer network computers aur devices ka ek set hai jo cables ya wireless links se jude hote hain, taaki wo data exchange kar sakein aur files, printers aur Internet connection jaise resources share kar sakein. <Cite id="networks-wireless-rainer-iis" /> <Cite id="networks-wireless-wiki-computer-network" />

**Applications**

1. **Resource sharing:** kai users ek printer, storage drive ya software licence share karte hain. Example: college lab mein ek printer.
2. **Information sharing:** files aur databases server pe rehte hain, to sabko same latest copy dikhti hai. Example: bank branch central database se balance padhti hai.
3. **Communication:** email, chat, voice aur video calls. Example: Pune aur London ke Infosys staff video pe meeting karte hain.
4. **E-commerce aur online services:** shopping, payments aur order tracking. Example: Amazon, Flipkart, UPI.
5. **Cloud computing aur remote work:** staff ghar se office systems use karta hai. Example: Zomato ka app cloud servers se baat karta hai.
6. **Banking aur finance:** ATMs, net banking aur cards bank ke central system se jude hote hain. Example: SBI ka ATM network.
7. **Education aur entertainment:** online classes, streaming, games. Example: NPTEL, Netflix.
8. **Backup aur control:** data doosri machine pe copy hota hai, aur administrator ek jagah se sabhi users ke liye rules set karta hai. <Cite id="networks-wireless-gfg-importance" /> <Cite id="networks-wireless-ibm-networking" />

**Disadvantages:** set-up cost, ek failure se kai users ruk sakte hain, aur virus tezi se phail sakta hai.

**Conclusion:** networks log aur systems ko resources aur information share karne dete hain, isliye har modern business inpe depend karta hai.
