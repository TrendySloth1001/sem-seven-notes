**In simple words:** information systems (IS) do not only help companies. They change how ordinary people live, learn, get health care and deal with the government. <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" loc="Ch. 1" />

**Importance of IS to society**

1. **Quality of life:** online shopping, banking, booking and government services save time and travel. Work from home gives flexibility.
2. **Robots and automation:** machines do dangerous, dirty or repetitive work, such as welding in car factories and picking items in warehouses.
3. **Health care:** electronic health records, medical imaging and online consultations (telehealth) help doctors decide faster and reach faraway patients. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-telehealth" />
4. **Education:** online courses such as NPTEL and SWAYAM reach anyone with internet.
5. **Access to information and voice:** search engines and social media let anyone find facts and share views. <Cite id="it-impact-society-gfg-media-society" />
6. **Government services:** online tax filing and UPI payments cut queues and paperwork.
7. **People with disabilities:** assistive technology helps them study, work and communicate.

**Problems IS creates**

- Job loss and the need to learn new skills.
- Privacy and security risks.
- The **digital divide**: people without devices, internet or skills fall behind. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-digital-divide" />
- Fake news, screen addiction and electronic waste.

**Conclusion:** IS is like electricity for a town. It improves life for most people, but society also needs safety rules and access for everyone.
