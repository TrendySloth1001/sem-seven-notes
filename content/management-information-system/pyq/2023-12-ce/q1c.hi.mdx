**In simple words:** business intelligence (BI) firm ke bikhre hue data ko reports aur dashboards mein badalta hai, taaki managers andaze ki jagah facts se decide karein.

**Definition:** BI applications, technologies aur processes ka ek set hai jo data ko collect, store, access aur analyse karta hai, taaki business users behtar decisions le sakein. <Cite id="business-intelligence-wiki-bi" /> <Cite id="business-intelligence-ibm-bi" />

**BI decision tak kaise pahunchta hai:** source systems (TPS, ERP, CRM) ETL (extract, clean, load) ko data dete hain, jo data warehouse bharta hai; OLAP aur data mining jaise tools use analyse karte hain; dashboards result dikhate hain; manager decide karta hai. <Cite id="business-intelligence-gfg-bi" />

**Decision making pe impact**

1. **Fact-based decisions:** data intuition ki jagah leta hai.
2. **Faster decisions:** data pehle se integrated hai, to jawab hafton ki jagah minutes mein aata hai.
3. **Business ka single view:** sales, finance aur stock ek saath dikhte hain.
4. **Behtar forecasting:** trends se demand aur budget plan karna aasaan hota hai.
5. **Early warning:** dashboards girti sales ya badhti cost pehle hi flag kar dete hain.
6. **Customer ki samajh:** data mining se buying patterns dikhte hain, jisse targeted offers ban sakte hain.
7. **Har level pe decisions:** self-service BI se sirf executives nahi, operational staff bhi decide kar sakte hain.
8. **Measurement:** KPIs dikhate hain ki decision kaam kiya ya nahi. <Cite id="business-intelligence-tp-bi" />

**Example:** ek retail chain ke dashboard mein dikhta hai ki North India mein winter jacket sales do hafte jaldi badh rahi hain. Manager turant southern stores se stock shift kar deta hai aur shortage se bach jaata hai.

**Limits:** kharab data quality galat jawab deti hai, cost zyada hai, bahut saare dashboards se overload hota hai, aur numbers human judgment ko ignore kar sakte hain.

**Conclusion:** BI decisions ko tez, behtar informed aur measurable banata hai, bas data clean ho aur managers judgment bhi use karein.
