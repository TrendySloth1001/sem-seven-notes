**In simple words:** har saal firm ki information pe attack karna aasaan aur use bachana mushkil hota ja raha hai. Rainer aur Prince paanch reasons batate hain. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-wiley-rainer" />

**Vulnerability** ka matlab hai wo kamzori jise koi threat use karke information resource ko nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-wiki-cybercrime" />

| # | Factor | Risk kyun badhta hai | Specific example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interconnected, interdependent, wirelessly networked business environment | Har connected device ya partner attacker ke liye ek possible darwaza hai | Shop ka payment system Internet se juda hai; kamzor supplier link se attacker ghus jaata hai |
| 2 | Smaller, faster, cheaper computers and storage devices | Bahut saara data pen drive ya phone mein aa jaata hai, isliye kho jaana ya chori hona aasaan hai | Customer records wala laptop gaadi se chori ho gaya |
| 3 | Decreasing skills needed to be a hacker | Ready-made attack tools online free milte hain, to beginner bhi attack kar sakta hai | Ek teenager download kiya hua password-cracking tool chalata hai |
| 4 | International organized crime taking over cybercrime | Paise wale gangs profit ke liye attack karte hain aur borders ke paar unhe trace karna mushkil hai | Ek gang ransomware se hospital ki files lock karke paisa maangta hai |
| 5 | Lack of management support | Budget aur policy ke bina basic protections skip ho jaate hain | Management patch late karta hai aur server breach ho jaata hai; WannaCry (2017) un systems pe laga jinhone available Windows patch install nahi kiya tha <Cite id="note-information-security-wiki-wannacry" /> |

<Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cybercrime" />

**Aur reasons jo aksar add kiye jaate hain:** cloud aur mobile use, remote work aur insiders ko zyada access.

**Conclusion:** zyada connection, chhote devices, aasaan tools, organized criminals aur kamzor management mil ke information resources ko har saal zyada exposed bana dete hain, isliye layered controls zaroori hain.
