**In simple words:** when a firm brings in computers, networks and software, it changes more than its tools. The structure of the firm, the jobs in it, the way managers decide and the health of workers all change. Think of a school that moves from a paper register to an attendance app: the principal sees absent students at once, and the clerk who compiled monthly reports now has a different job. <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" loc="Ch. 1" />

**Definition:** the impact of IT on organizations means the changes that IT brings to how a firm is structured, how its managers and employees work, and how safe and healthy its workplace is.

**Main impacts**

1. **Flatter structure:** a system can collect and summarize reports that middle managers used to pass upward, so there are fewer layers and each manager can supervise more people. The effect is mixed in practice, so write "IT tends to flatten organizations".
2. **Changed manager's job:** managers get fast, detailed information, make quicker decisions and can lead remote teams by email and video.
3. **Jobs lost and created:** routine work such as data entry gets automated, while new jobs appear for developers, data analysts and security staff.
4. **Health and comfort:** long screen hours cause eye strain, back pain and repetitive strain injury. Good ergonomics (chairs, screens and keyboards that fit the body) reduces this. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-ergonomics" />
5. **Stress and monitoring:** being always reachable and being tracked can add stress.
6. **Inclusion:** screen readers and work from home open jobs to people with disabilities.

| Area | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Flatter, faster communication | Fewer middle-manager posts |
| Decisions | Real-time information | Information overload |
| Jobs | New skilled jobs | Routine jobs automated |
| Health | Less heavy manual work | Eye strain, back pain, stress |

**Example:** Infosys and SBI use shared systems so that head office sees branch figures the same day. <Cite id="it-impact-society-gfg-mis" />

**Conclusion:** IT makes organizations faster and flatter, but managers must plan for retraining, ergonomics and stress along with the benefits.
