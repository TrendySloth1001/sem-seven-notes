**In simple words:** jab koi firm computers, networks aur software laati hai, to sirf tools nahi badalte. Firm ka structure, jobs, managers ke decisions lene ka tarika aur workers ki health, sab badal jaata hai. Socho ek school paper register chhodkar attendance app use karta hai: principal ko absent students turant dikh jaate hain, aur jo clerk monthly report banata tha uska kaam badal jaata hai. <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" loc="Ch. 1" />

**Definition:** impact of IT on organizations ka matlab hai wo changes jo IT firm ke structure, managers aur employees ke kaam karne ke tarike, aur workplace ki safety aur health mein laati hai.

**Main impacts**

1. **Flatter structure:** system reports ko collect aur summarize kar deta hai, jo pehle middle managers upar bhejte the. Isliye layers kam hoti hain aur ek manager zyada logon ko sambhal sakta hai. Practice mein result mixed hota hai, isliye likho "IT tends to flatten organizations".
2. **Manager ka job badalta hai:** managers ko fast aur detailed information milti hai, decisions jaldi hote hain, aur wo email aur video se door baithe teams ko lead kar sakte hain.
3. **Jobs jaati bhi hain, aati bhi hain:** data entry jaisa routine kaam automate ho jaata hai, aur developers, data analysts aur security staff ke naye jobs bante hain.
4. **Health aur comfort:** screen pe zyada der baithne se eye strain, back pain aur repetitive strain injury hoti hai. Achhi ergonomics (body ke hisaab se chair, screen aur keyboard) isse kam karti hai. <Cite id="it-impact-society-wiki-ergonomics" />
5. **Stress aur monitoring:** hamesha available rehna aur track hona stress badhata hai.
6. **Inclusion:** screen readers aur work from home se disabled logon ko bhi jobs milti hain.

| Area | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Flatter, faster communication | Fewer middle-manager posts |
| Decisions | Real-time information | Information overload |
| Jobs | New skilled jobs | Routine jobs automated |
| Health | Less heavy manual work | Eye strain, back pain, stress |

**Example:** Infosys aur SBI shared systems use karte hain, jisse head office ko branch ke figures usi din mil jaate hain. <Cite id="it-impact-society-gfg-mis" />

**Conclusion:** IT organizations ko faster aur flatter banati hai, lekin managers ko benefits ke saath retraining, ergonomics aur stress ka bhi plan karna padta hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "IT tends to flatten organizations, changes managers' jobs, creates and removes jobs, and affects employee health."
