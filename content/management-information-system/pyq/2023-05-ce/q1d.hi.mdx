**In simple words:** company ka social media account ab wo complaint desk hai jise poori duniya dekh sakti hai. Public mein jaldi aur politely reply dena baaki sab customers ko batata hai ki firm ko parwah hai. Social computing ka matlab hai social networks, chat apps aur forums jaise web tools ka use, jahan log interact karte aur share karte hain. <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-social-computing" />

**Social computing customer service ko kaise badal rahi hai**

1. **Fast, public support:** customer problem post karta hai aur firm reply deti hai. Indian Railways ke passengers X pe train aur coach ki problems batate hain aur railway ke service accounts jawab dete hain.
2. **Chatbots se 24 x 7 help:** WhatsApp, Messenger aur websites pe bots order status jaise simple sawaalon ka jawab dete hain. SBI balance aur mini-statement ke liye WhatsApp banking deta hai. <Cite id="crm-wiki-chatbot" />
3. **Customer communities aur forums:** dusre customers sawaalon ka jawab dete hain, isse support team ka load kam hota hai.
4. **Social listening:** firm apne brand name ko search karti hai aur complaints dhoondh leti hai, chahe kisi ne tag na kiya ho.
5. **Self-help content:** FAQs, how-to videos aur wikis se customers khud problem solve kar lete hain.
6. **Feedback loop:** reviews aur complaints batate hain ki product mein kya theek karna hai.
7. **Personal touch aur CRM link:** staff pehle ke posts dekhkar friendly tarike se jawab de sakta hai, aur complaint CRM system mein service ticket ban jaati hai (social CRM). <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-social-crm" />

| Benefit | Risk |
|---|---|
| Quick, low-cost support | Kharab jawab sabko dikhta hai |
| Problems jaldi pakdi jaati hain | Customers instant reply expect karte hain |
| Trust aur loyalty banti hai | Messages ka volume sambhalna mushkil |

**Example:** Zomato aur Amazon social media aur chat pe complaints ka jawab dete hain aur feedback se service improve karte hain. <Cite id="crm-gfg-intro" />

**Conclusion:** social computing customer service ko open, two-way conversation bana deti hai, aur agar firm tez aur achha reply de to loyalty badhti hai.
