**In simple words:** haan. Jo company doosron ka data rakhti hai wo locker attendant jaisi hai jiske paas aapki valuables hain. "Lock karna bahut kaam hai" kehkar locker khula chhodna galat hai, chahe koi rule force na kare. Security ek duty hai, sirf technical kaam nahi. <Cite id="ethics-privacy-rainer-ch3" />

**Definition:** ethical responsibility matlab sahi karne aur doosron ko nuksaan na pahunchane ki duty. Information security information ko unauthorized access, change ya loss se bachana hai. Dono milkar: jo organization data collect karti hai use protect karna chahiye.

**Security ethical responsibility kyun hai**

1. **Duty of care:** data customers ka hai, jo firm par bharosa karke de dete hain.
2. **Real logon ko harm:** leak se fraud, identity theft, stress aur paise ka loss hota hai.
3. **Privacy security par depend karti hai:** data secure nahi hai to private bhi nahi ho sakta.
4. **Accountability:** firm ko failures ka jawab dena hoga, unhe honestly aur jaldi report karna hoga aur theek karna hoga.
5. **Society mein trust:** log digital services tabhi use karte hain jab unhe data safe lagta hai. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-pearson-laudon" /> <Cite id="ethics-privacy-gfg-ethical-issues" />

**Case study: Equifax breach, 2017.** Equifax ek badi US credit reporting company hai. Apache Struts web software ke flaw ka fix 7 March 2017 ko release hua, lekin Equifax ne use apni ek website par apply nahi kiya. Attackers ne May 2017 se flaw use kiya, aur breach 76 din tak notice nahi hua. Lagbhag 147.9 million logon ke names, birth dates, social security numbers aur addresses expose hue. <Cite id="note-information-security-wiki-equifax" />

Consequences: share price ek din mein lagbhag 13 percent gira, CEO ne retire kiya, aur 2019 mein Equifax US regulators aur states ke saath kam se kam lagbhag USD 575 million ke settlements par agree hui. <Cite id="ethics-privacy-ftc-equifax" />

**Ethical lessons:** responsibility ka failure (patch nahi kiya), accountability ka failure (late detection aur disclosure) aur liability ka exposure (penalties aur claims). Jin logon ko harm hua unhone Equifax ko data diya hi nahi tha, isliye duty aur strong ho jaati hai.

| Without ethical security | With ethical security |
|---|---|
| Fraud and identity theft for customers | Safe, trusting customers |
| Fines, lawsuits, lost reputation for the firm | Trust and loyalty |

**Conclusion:** security ek ethical responsibility hai, kyunki data protect karna logon ko protect karna hai, aur Equifax case ignore karne ki cost dikhata hai.
