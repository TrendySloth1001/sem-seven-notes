**In simple words:** an Executive Support System (ESS) is a system that gives the top managers of a company a quick, simple view of the most important figures, and lets them look deeper when something seems wrong. It is like the dashboard of a car for the CEO. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-wiki-eis" />

**Definition:** an ESS is a computer-based system that gives senior executives easy access to key internal and external information, so they can monitor the organization and take strategic decisions. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-tp-ess" />

**Features**

1. **Made for top management:** supports strategic, non-routine decisions.
2. **Easy to use:** graphs, menus and simple screens, so executives need no technical skill.
3. **Drill-down:** click a summary figure (company profit) to see the detail behind it (region, then branch).
4. **Key indicators on a dashboard:** revenue, profit and market share on one screen instead of long reports.
5. **Internal and external data:** combines company summaries with outside data such as competitor news, markets and government policy.
6. **Trend analysis:** compares figures over time to spot trends.
7. **Communication support:** often has email, scheduling and conferencing.
8. **Timely information:** up-to-date data for quick decisions. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-gfg-types" />

**Example:** the CEO of an e-commerce firm such as Flipkart sees sales, profit and delivery time on one dashboard, finds one city with high returns and drills down to the cause.

**Limits:** costly, may cause information overload, and its benefits are hard to measure.

**Conclusion:** an ESS gives executives a simple, summarized and drillable view of internal and external data for strategic decisions.
