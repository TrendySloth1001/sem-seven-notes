**In simple words:** yes, blogs and wikis are different. A blog is a place where one person (or a small team) tells something and readers comment. A wiki is a set of pages that many people write and improve together. Blog is for telling, wiki is for building together. <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-blog" /> <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-wiki" />

**Definitions**

- **Blog:** a website of dated posts, newest first, written by the owner. Readers can comment but cannot change the post.
- **Wiki:** a website whose pages can be edited by many permitted users, with every change saved in a history.

| Point | Blog | Wiki |
|---|---|---|
| Authors | One person or small team | Many contributors |
| Who edits the main text | Only the author; readers comment | Anyone with permission |
| Order | Dated posts, newest first | Linked pages, no date order |
| Voice | Personal opinion and story | Neutral, shared knowledge |
| Purpose | Telling, marketing, building followers | Building knowledge together |
| Version history | Not the main feature | Every edit saved, can be undone |
| Example | WordPress, Blogger | Wikipedia, Confluence |

**Application of a blog:** a company blog, such as a bank sharing news and tips about savings and loans, attracts customers and builds trust.

**Application of a wiki:** an internal wiki at an IT firm such as Infosys, where staff keep manuals, FAQs and project notes that everyone can update. <Cite id="social-computing-gfg-web-versions" />

**Conclusion:** both are Web 2.0 tools, but a blog is one-to-many publishing and a wiki is many-to-many editing.
