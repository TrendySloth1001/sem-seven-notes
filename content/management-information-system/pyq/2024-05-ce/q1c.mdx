**In simple words:** information security means keeping information safe from the wrong people, from being changed, and from being lost, while keeping it ready for the right people. Like a bank locker: only you can open it, nothing inside is changed, and it is there whenever you need it. <Cite id="note-information-security-ibm-infosec" />

**Definition:** information security is the protection of information and information systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification or destruction, so that the information stays confidential, correct and available. <Cite id="note-information-security-nist-csf" />

**The three goals (CIA triad)**

1. **Confidentiality:** only authorized people can see the information.
2. **Integrity:** the information is accurate and changed only by authorized people.
3. **Availability:** authorized users can use the information when they need it. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cia" />

**Example: SBI net banking.** The customer logs in with a user ID, password and an OTP, which proves identity (confidentiality). Data travels over an encrypted HTTPS link, and the bank checks that the amount is not altered (integrity). A backup server keeps the service running if one server fails (availability). All of these together are information security. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-infosec" />

**Why it is needed:** information is a valuable asset, and its loss means money loss, legal trouble and lost customer trust.

**Conclusion:** information security protects confidentiality, integrity and availability of information, using people, process and technology together.
