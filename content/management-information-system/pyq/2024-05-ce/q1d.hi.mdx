**In simple words:** haan, blogs aur wikis alag hain. Blog ek jagah hai jahan ek person (ya chhoti team) kuch batata hai aur readers comment karte hain. Wiki pages ka ek set hai jise bahut saare log milkar likhte aur improve karte hain. Blog batane ke liye hai, wiki milkar banane ke liye. <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-blog" /> <Cite id="social-computing-wiki-wiki" />

**Definitions**

- **Blog:** dated posts ki website, newest first, jo owner likhta hai. Readers comment kar sakte hain par post change nahi kar sakte.
- **Wiki:** aisi website jiske pages bahut saare permitted users edit kar sakte hain, aur har change history mein save hota hai.

| Point | Blog | Wiki |
|---|---|---|
| Authors | One person or small team | Many contributors |
| Who edits the main text | Only the author; readers comment | Anyone with permission |
| Order | Dated posts, newest first | Linked pages, no date order |
| Voice | Personal opinion and story | Neutral, shared knowledge |
| Purpose | Telling, marketing, building followers | Building knowledge together |
| Version history | Not the main feature | Every edit saved, can be undone |
| Example | WordPress, Blogger | Wikipedia, Confluence |

**Blog ka application:** company blog, jaise bank savings aur loans ke news aur tips share karta hai, customers ko attract karta hai aur trust banata hai.

**Wiki ka application:** Infosys jaisi IT firm mein internal wiki, jahan staff manuals, FAQs aur project notes rakhta hai jo sab update kar sakte hain. <Cite id="social-computing-gfg-web-versions" />

**Conclusion:** dono Web 2.0 tools hain, lekin blog one-to-many publishing hai aur wiki many-to-many editing hai.
