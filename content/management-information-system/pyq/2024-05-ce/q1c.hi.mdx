**In simple words:** information security ka matlab hai information ko galat logon se, change hone se aur kho jaane se bachana, aur sahi logon ke liye ready rakhna. Bank locker jaisa: sirf aap khol sakte ho, andar kuch badla nahi jaata, aur jab chahiye tab mil jaata hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-ibm-infosec" />

**Definition:** information security information aur information systems ko unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification ya destruction se bachane ka naam hai, taaki information confidential, correct aur available rahe. <Cite id="note-information-security-nist-csf" />

**The three goals (CIA triad)**

1. **Confidentiality:** sirf authorized log information dekh sakte hain.
2. **Integrity:** information accurate rahti hai aur sirf authorized log hi use change kar sakte hain.
3. **Availability:** authorized users ko jab zaroorat ho tab information mil jaati hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cia" />

**Example: SBI net banking.** Customer user ID, password aur OTP se login karta hai, jo identity prove karta hai (confidentiality). Data encrypted HTTPS link par jaata hai, aur bank check karta hai ki amount change nahi hua (integrity). Ek server fail ho to backup server service chalu rakhta hai (availability). Ye sab milkar information security hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-infosec" />

**Ye kyun zaroori hai:** information ek valuable asset hai, aur iske loss ka matlab hai paisa loss, legal trouble aur customer ka bharosa tootna.

**Conclusion:** information security people, process aur technology ko saath use karke information ki confidentiality, integrity aur availability ko protect karti hai.
