**In simple words:** online shop customer ko simple lagti hai, lekin screen ke peeche bahut records sahi rakhne padte hain: products, stock, payments, delivery aur customers. MIS ye data collect, store aur report karta hai, aur website ko warehouse, accounts aur delivery se jodta hai. <Cite id="e-commerce-rainer-intro-is" /> <Cite id="e-commerce-laudon-mis" />

**How MIS supports e-commerce**

| Business need | What the MIS does |
|---|---|
| Show products | Product database with price, photos, stock and reviews |
| Take orders and payment | Records the order, links to the payment gateway, avoids double payment |
| Manage stock | Tracks every item in every warehouse; warns when stock is low |
| Deliver | Picks the nearest warehouse and courier; gives tracking |
| Know the customer | Customer data gives history, offers and recommendations |
| Decide | Reports: sales by day, returns, late deliveries |

**Case: Flipkart-style shop**

1. Customer phone search karta hai. Product database price aur live stock dikhata hai, aur recommendation system accessories suggest karta hai.
2. Customer UPI se pay karta hai. System order aur payment record karta hai.
3. Inventory system stock kam karta hai aur nearest warehouse chunta hai. Stock low ho to supplier ko purchase order jaata hai.
4. Logistics system courier assign karta hai aur tracking updates bhejta hai.
5. Reports manager ko best sellers aur late deliveries dikhati hain, taaki action liya ja sake.

*Result:* faster delivery, kam stock-outs aur better decisions. Amazon bhi yahi tareeka bade scale par use karta hai. <Cite id="e-commerce-ibm" />

**Conclusion:** MIS wo backbone hai jo customer-facing site ko stock, payment, delivery aur management reports se jodta hai. <Cite id="e-commerce-gfg-intro" />
