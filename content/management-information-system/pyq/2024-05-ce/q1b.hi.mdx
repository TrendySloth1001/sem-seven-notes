**In simple words:** Executive Support System (ESS) ek aisa system hai jo company ke top managers ko sabse important figures ka quick aur simple view deta hai, aur kuch galat lage to aur gehraai mein dekhne deta hai. Ye CEO ke liye car ke dashboard jaisa hai. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-wiki-eis" />

**Definition:** ESS ek computer-based system hai jo senior executives ko key internal aur external information tak easy access deta hai, taaki wo organization ko monitor kar sakein aur strategic decisions le sakein. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-tp-ess" />

**Features**

1. **Top management ke liye bana:** strategic, non-routine decisions ko support karta hai.
2. **Easy to use:** graphs, menus aur simple screens, isliye executives ko technical skill nahi chahiye.
3. **Drill-down:** summary figure (company profit) par click karke uske peeche ki detail (region, phir branch) dekh sakte hain.
4. **Dashboard par key indicators:** lambi reports ki jagah revenue, profit aur market share ek screen par.
5. **Internal aur external data:** company ke summaries ke saath bahar ka data, jaise competitor news, markets aur government policy.
6. **Trend analysis:** time ke saath figures compare karke trends pakadta hai.
7. **Communication support:** aksar email, scheduling aur conferencing hoti hai.
8. **Timely information:** quick decisions ke liye up-to-date data. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-gfg-types" />

**Example:** Flipkart jaisi e-commerce firm ka CEO ek dashboard par sales, profit aur delivery time dekhta hai, ek city mein high returns paata hai aur drill-down karke wajah tak pahunchta hai.

**Limits:** costly hai, information overload ho sakta hai, aur iske benefits napna mushkil hai.

**Conclusion:** ESS executives ko internal aur external data ka simple, summarized aur drillable view deta hai strategic decisions ke liye.
