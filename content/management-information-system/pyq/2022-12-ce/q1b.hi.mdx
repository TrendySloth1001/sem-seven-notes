**In simple words:** data governance matlab company ke data ke liye clear rules aur owners tay karna, taaki data correct, safe ho aur har jagah ek hi tarah se use ho. Ye shehar ke traffic rules jaisa hai: sirf roads kaafi nahi hoti. <Cite id="data-management-ibm-data-governance" />

**Definition:** data governance policies, standards, roles aur processes ka set hai jo tay karta hai ki poore organization mein data kaise create, store, use, protect aur retire hoga. <Cite id="data-management-wiki-data-governance" />

**MIS mein ye kaise achieve hota hai, step by step**

1. **Goals set karo:** jaise "billing errors kam karna" ya "privacy audit pass karna".
2. **Roles assign karo:** upar steering committee, aur har data area ke liye data owner (business manager) aur data steward (roz ki dekhbhal).
3. **Policies aur standards likho:** common definitions jaise "active customer kise kahenge", naming aur format rules, access rules, data kitne time tak rakhna hai.
4. **Data catalog banao:** ek jagah jahan likha ho ki kaunsa data hai aur uska matlab kya hai.
5. **Master data aur quality manage karo:** har customer ya product ka ek trusted "golden record" rakho aur data ko regularly clean karo. <Cite id="data-management-gfg-mdm" />
6. **Access aur security control karo:** har role ko sirf wahi data dikhe jo use chahiye.
7. **Lineage track karo aur audit karo:** record rakho ki data kahan se aaya, aur check karo ki rules follow ho rahe hain.

| Role | Kaam |
|---|---|
| Steering committee | Strategy set karti hai aur disputes settle karti hai |
| Data owner | Ek data area ke liye accountable |
| Data steward | Roz rules apply karta hai |

**Example:** SBI jaisa bank ek hi customer record rakhta hai jo har branch aur app use karti hai, aur sirf authorised staff use khol sakta hai.

**Conclusion:** governance se MIS ko one version of the truth, behtar decisions aur data leak ka kam risk milta hai.
