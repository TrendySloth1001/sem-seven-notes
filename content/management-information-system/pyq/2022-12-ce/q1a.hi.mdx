**In simple words:** MIS ek aisa system hai jo business ke roz ke data ko managers ke liye chhoti, useful reports mein badal deta hai. MIS ko teen tareeke se group kar sakte hain: kis management level ko serve karta hai, kis tarah ki report deta hai, aur kis department ko serve karta hai. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-gfg-mis" />

**Definition:** Management Information System (MIS) ek planned system hai jo information collect, store aur send karta hai, taaki managers plan, control aur decide kar sakein. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-tp-mis" />

**Management level ke hisaab se information systems ke types** (usual textbook answer)

| Type | Kaun use karta hai | Kya karta hai |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction processing system (TPS) | Clerks, operational staff | Bill aur booking jaise roz ke transactions record karta hai |
| Management information system (MIS) | Middle managers | Control ke liye summary aur exception reports deta hai |
| Decision support system (DSS) | Middle aur senior managers | Models se "what if" sawaalon ke jawab deta hai |
| Executive support system (ESS) | Senior executives | Long-term decisions ke liye key figures ka dashboard |

**Report ke hisaab se MIS ke types** <Cite id="is-fundamentals-gfg-types" />

| Type | Kya deta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Periodic (scheduled) report | Fixed time pe, fixed format mein banti hai | Region ke hisaab se monthly sales |
| Exception report | Sirf unusual cases jin pe action chahiye | Wo items jinka stock reorder level se neeche hai |
| Demand (on-demand) report | Manager maange tab banti hai | Ek product ki aaj ki sales |
| Ad-hoc report | Special sawaal ke liye nayi, one-time report | Pichhle saal monsoon mein umbrella sales |

**Function (department) ke hisaab se MIS ke types**

1. **Finance and accounting MIS:** budgets, cash flow, profit reports.
2. **Marketing MIS:** sales trends, customer aur market reports.
3. **Human resource MIS:** attendance, payroll, staff turnover.
4. **Production and operations MIS:** stock levels, production schedule, quality reports.

Inme se har ek poore MIS ka subsystem hai aur ek hi central database se data leta hai. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-wiki-mis" />

**Example:** Flipkart ke manager ko roz city ke hisaab se orders ki report milti hai (periodic), aur jab kisi warehouse mein popular phone khatam hota hai to alert aata hai (exception).

**Conclusion:** type kuch bhi ho, MIS sahi manager ko sahi summary sahi time pe deta hai.
