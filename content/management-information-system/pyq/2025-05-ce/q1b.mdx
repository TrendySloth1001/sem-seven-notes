**In simple words:** information security means keeping a company's information safe, so that only the right people can see it, nobody can secretly change it, and it is there when needed. It is like the locks, guards and spare keys of a bank for its data. <Cite id="note-information-security-ibm-infosec" />

**Definition:** information security is the protection of information and information systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification or destruction, so that the information stays confidential, correct and available. <Cite id="note-information-security-nist-csf" />

**The three goals (CIA triad)** <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cia" />

| Goal | Meaning | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Only authorized people see the data | Passwords, encryption |
| Integrity | Data stays correct and unaltered | Checks, audit logs |
| Availability | Data and service work when needed | Backups, spare servers |

**Example:** an SBI customer logs in to net banking. The bank asks for a user ID, password and OTP (confidentiality). It sends data over encrypted HTTPS and checks that the amount is not changed on the way (integrity). It keeps a backup server so the service runs even if one server fails (availability). All of this together is information security. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-infosec" />

**Why needed:** information is a valuable asset, and its loss means money loss, legal trouble and lost customers.

**Conclusion:** information security protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information.
