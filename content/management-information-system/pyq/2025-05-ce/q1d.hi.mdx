**In simple words:** system development naya information system banane ya purane ko change karne ka kaam hai, taaki wo business ki asli zaroorat solve kare. Ghar banane ki tarah ismein plan, design, construction, checking aur handover chahiye. <Cite id="note-sdlc-gfg-sdlc" />

**Definition:** system development business ki zaroorat ke hisaab se information system banane ya badalne ka process hai. System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) ise karne ka traditional, step-by-step structure hai. <Cite id="note-sdlc-wiki-sdlc" />

**Planned method kyun zaroori hai:** plan ke bina projects late hote hain, zyada paisa lete hain, ya aisa kuch banate hain jo kisi ko chahiye hi nahi tha. <Cite id="note-sdlc-ibm-sdlc" />

**SDLC ke six phases** <Cite id="it-impact-society-rainer-iis7" />

1. **Systems investigation:** system kyun chahiye, aur kya ye feasible hai?
2. **Systems analysis:** system ko kya karna hai?
3. **Systems design:** wo kaise karega?
4. **Programming and testing:** system banao aur check karo.
5. **Implementation:** go live, purane system se switch, users ko training.
6. **Operation and maintenance:** chalao, errors theek karo aur improve karo.

**System kaise acquire hota hai:** ready package kharido (Tally, SAP), SaaS ki tarah rent karo (Salesforce), in-house banao, vendor ko outsource karo (Infosys, TCS), open source use karo, ya end users khud chhote tools banayein.

**Kaun hissa leta hai:** users, managers, systems analysts (business aur technical logon ke beech ka bridge), programmers, testers aur ek project manager.

**Conclusion:** SDLC ke through organised system development pakka karta hai ki sahi system time par aur cost ke andar bane.
