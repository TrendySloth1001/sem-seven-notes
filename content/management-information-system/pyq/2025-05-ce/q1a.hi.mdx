**In simple words:** Executive Support System (ESS) company ke top bosses ko ek easy screen par sabse zaroori kuch figures dikhata hai. Isse wo dekh sakte hain ki company kaisi chal rahi hai aur bade decisions le sakte hain. Ye pilot ke cockpit display jaisa hai: kuch saaf dials, aur kisi ek ko paas se dekh sakte ho. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-tp-ess" />

**Definition:** ESS (jise Executive Information System bhi kehte hain) ek computer-based system hai jo senior executives ko summarised internal aur external information tak jaldi aur aasaan access deta hai, taaki wo organization ko monitor kar sakein aur strategic decisions le sakein. <Cite id="is-fundamentals-wiki-eis" />

**Features**

1. **Top management ke liye bana hai:** strategic, unstructured decisions ko support karta hai.
2. **Use karna aasaan:** graphs, menus aur dashboard; computer skill ki zaroorat nahi.
3. **Drill-down:** ek total pe click karke region, branch aur single figures tak neeche ja sakte ho.
4. **Key indicators:** lambi reports ki jagah revenue, profit aur market share jaise summarised figures dikhata hai.
5. **Internal aur external data:** company ke summaries ko market news, competitor aur government information ke saath jodta hai.
6. **Trend analysis:** time ke saath figures compare karke problems jaldi pakadta hai.
7. **Communication support:** email, alerts aur scheduling tools.
8. **Kam modelling:** ye business ko dekhne ke liye hai, heavy what-if analysis ke liye nahi (wo DSS ka kaam hai).

**Example:** ek retail chain ka CEO aaj ki company sales ek screen par dekhta hai, sabse weak city par click karta hai, phir sabse weak store par, aur wajah dhoondh leta hai. <Cite id="business-intelligence-tp-ess" />

**Limits:** costly hai, iska benefit naapna mushkil hai, aur data hamesha current hona chahiye.

**Conclusion:** ESS data ko strategic control ke liye ek simple, top-level picture mein badal deta hai.
