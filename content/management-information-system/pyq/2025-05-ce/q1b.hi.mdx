**In simple words:** information security ka matlab hai company ki information ko safe rakhna, taaki sirf sahi log use dekh sakein, koi chupke se change na kar sake, aur zaroorat ke time wo mil jaye. Ye bank ke data ke liye locks, guards aur spare keys jaisa hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-ibm-infosec" />

**Definition:** information security information aur information systems ko unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification ya destruction se bachana hai, taaki information confidential, correct aur available rahe. <Cite id="note-information-security-nist-csf" />

**Teen goals (CIA triad)** <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-cia" />

| Goal | Meaning | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Sirf authorized log data dekhte hain | Passwords, encryption |
| Integrity | Data sahi aur unaltered rehta hai | Checks, audit logs |
| Availability | Zaroorat par data aur service chalti hai | Backups, spare servers |

**Example:** ek SBI customer net banking mein login karta hai. Bank user ID, password aur OTP maangta hai (confidentiality). Data encrypted HTTPS par jaata hai aur check hota hai ki raste mein amount change na ho (integrity). Bank ek backup server rakhta hai taaki ek server fail ho to bhi service chale (availability). Ye sab milkar information security hai. <Cite id="note-information-security-gfg-infosec" />

**Kyun zaroori hai:** information ek valuable asset hai, aur uske loss ka matlab hai paise ka nuksaan, legal problem aur customers ka khona.

**Conclusion:** information security information ki confidentiality, integrity aur availability ko protect karti hai.
