**In simple words:** topology network ki shape hai: computers aur devices kaise arrange hain aur ek doosre se kaise jude hain. Ye shehar ke road map jaisa hai, jo decide karta hai ki traffic kaise chalega. <Cite id="networks-wireless-wiki-topology" />

**Definition:** network topology network ke devices (nodes) aur links ki arrangement hai. Ye physical ho sakti hai (cables kaise bichhe hain) ya logical (data kaise flow karta hai). <Cite id="networks-wireless-ibm-topology" />

**Types, advantages aur disadvantages ke saath** <Cite id="networks-wireless-gfg-topology" /> <Cite id="networks-wireless-tp-topologies" />

| Type | Kaise connected | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bus | Saare devices ek shared main cable par | Sasta; kam cable | Ek cable toote to sab band; users zyada hon to slow |
| Star | Har device ek central switch se juda | Device add karna aasaan; ek kharab cable sirf ek device ko affect karta hai | Switch fail ho to sab band |
| Ring | Closed loop; har device ke do neighbours | Data flow orderly; equal access | Ek break se ring ruk sakti hai; device add karna mushkil |
| Mesh | Devices kai ya sabse jude | Bahut reliable; kai paths | Bahut costly; bahut cables |
| Tree | Stars ek parent se levels mein jude | Groups mein badhana aasaan | Root device par depend |
| Hybrid | Do ya zyada types mix | Flexible; need ke hisaab se | Complex aur costly |

**Use:** college lab aam taur par star network use karti hai (har room mein ek switch); internet backbone reliability ke liye partial mesh use karta hai.

**Conclusion:** choice cost, reliability aur size par depend karti hai; offices mein star sabse common hai.
