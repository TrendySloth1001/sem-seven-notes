**In simple words:** collaborative filtering aapko movie isliye recommend karta hai kyunki aapke jaise taste wale logon ko wo pasand aayi. Use movie ke baare mein kuch jaanne ki zaroorat nahi, sirf ye ki kisne kya rate kiya. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-cf" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-google-cf" />

**Steps (user-based)**

1. **Utility matrix** banao: rows users, columns movies, entries ratings; zyadatar khali hoti hain.
2. Target user aur har dusre user ki **similarity** nikalo, un movies se jo dono ne rate ki hain (cosine similarity, ya har user ki average rating ghatane ke baad Pearson correlation).
3. Sabse similar users (neighbours) chuno.
4. Missing rating ko neighbours ki us movie ki ratings ke similarity-weighted average se **predict** karo.
5. Sabse high predicted rating wali movies recommend karo. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-av" />

<Sum>

<Given>

Ratings (1 se 5); "?" matlab rate nahi kiya.

| | M1 | M2 | M3 | M4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | 5 | 3 | ? | 4 |
| U2 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| U3 | 1 | 5 | 2 | 1 |

</Given>

<Find>

U1 ki M3 ke liye predicted rating, dono users ne jo movies rate ki hain unpar cosine similarity se.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. U1 ke saath U2 aur U3 ki **common movies** M1, M2, M4 hain. U1 = (5, 3, 4), U2 = (4, 2, 4), U3 = (1, 5, 1).
2. **cos(U1, U2).** Dot product $= 20 + 6 + 16 = 42$. Lengths: $\sqrt{25+9+16} = \sqrt{50} = 7.071$ aur $\sqrt{16+4+16} = 6$. To $42 / (7.071 \times 6) = 0.990$.
3. **cos(U1, U3).** Dot product $= 5 + 15 + 4 = 24$. U3 ki length $= \sqrt{1+25+1} = \sqrt{27} = 5.196$. To $24 / (7.071 \times 5.196) = 0.653$.
4. Unki M3 ratings (U2 ne 5, U3 ne 2) ka **weighted average**: $(0.990 \times 5 + 0.653 \times 2) / (0.990 + 0.653) = 6.256 / 1.643 = 3.81$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

U1 ke liye M3 ki predicted rating $\approx 3.81$, to M3 achhi recommendation hai (beech wali rating 3 se upar).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Weights U2 ke 0.602 aur U3 ke 0.398 hain, to $0.602 \times 5 + 0.398 \times 2 = 3.01 + 0.80 = 3.81$, wahi value. Ye 2 aur 5 ke beech hai aur 5 ke paas, kyunki U2 U1 se zyada similar hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Types aur limits.** Item-based filtering users ke bajay movies ko compare karta hai aur bade systems mein zyada stable hai. Collaborative filtering mein **cold start** problem (naye users ya movies ki ratings nahi), **sparsity** (kam ratings) aur scale ki dikkat hai. Raw ratings pe cosine ye ignore karta hai ki kuch users high rate karte hain aur kuch low; Pearson ise theek karta hai. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-pearson" />

**Conclusion:** collaborative filtering user ki rating ko similar users ki ratings se predict karta hai, jaise Netflix-style movie recommendation.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Collaborative filtering predicts a user's rating for an item from the ratings of similar users."
