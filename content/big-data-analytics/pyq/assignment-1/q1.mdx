**In simple words:** big data means data so large, fast and varied that one normal database cannot store or process it. Phones, websites, sensors and payments create it every second, and companies use it to take better decisions. <Cite id="big-data-intro-wiki" /> <Cite id="big-data-intro-ibm" />

**Why it is significant today** (each point is one reason a student can write):

1. **Improved decision making.** Companies decide using facts from data, not guesses. **Example:** a retailer checks last year's sales by city and stocks more of what sells.
2. **Customer understanding.** Clicks, searches and purchases show what people like and why they leave. **Example:** an online shop finds that many users drop out at the payment page and fixes it.
3. **Personalised services.** Each user gets suggestions made for them. **Example:** Netflix and YouTube recommend videos from your watch history. <Cite id="big-data-intro-av" />
4. **Fraud detection.** Unusual patterns in millions of transactions are caught at once. **Example:** a bank blocks a card used in two countries within one hour.
5. **Healthcare.** Patient records, scans and wearable readings help find disease early and choose treatment. **Example:** a smartwatch warns of an irregular heartbeat.
6. **Real-time applications.** Fast data gives instant answers. **Example:** Google Maps shows live traffic and a new route.
7. **Business growth.** Better products, targeted ads and lower cost raise profit. **Example:** an airline sets ticket prices from demand data.

**Extend in the exam (add these to reach 10 marks):**

- **Characteristics (5 V's).** Volume (amount), velocity (speed), variety (formats), veracity (trust), value (usefulness). <Cite id="big-data-intro-tp" />
- **Types of data.** Structured (bank tables), semi-structured (JSON, logs) and unstructured (photos, video, posts).
- **Applications by field.** Retail (recommendations), finance (fraud), manufacturing (machine failure prediction), transport (route planning), telecom, government. <Cite id="web-bda-1-gcloud-bigdata" />
- **Challenges.** Storing huge data, poor quality, privacy and security rules.

**Conclusion:** big data is significant because it turns the huge data of the digital world into better decisions, safer services and growth.
