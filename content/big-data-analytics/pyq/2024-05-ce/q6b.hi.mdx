**In simple words:** collaborative filtering (CF) user ko wo items recommend karta hai jo **doosre similar users** ko pasand aaye. Use items ke baare mein kuch jaanne ki zaroorat nahi, sirf ye ki kisne kya rate kiya. Idea: jo log pehle agree karte the, wo aage bhi agree karenge. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-mmds-book" loc="§9.3" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-google-cf" />

**Data jo use hota hai:** **utility matrix**, jisme users rows hain, items columns hain aur ratings cells hain. Zyadatar cells khali hote hain, kyunki user sirf kuch items rate karta hai.

**Types**

1. **User-user CF:** target user jaise users dhundho, phir unki ratings se rating predict karo.
2. **Item-item CF:** un items jaise items dhundho jo user ko pasand aaye (jo items same logon ne ek jaisa rate kiye). Amazon ne isse use kiya. Ye zyada stable hai, kyunki items ki taste users ki taste se kam badalti hai. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-linden2003" />

**Steps (user-user)**

1. Utility matrix banao.
2. Cosine similarity ya Pearson correlation se users ke beech similarity naapo.
3. Nearest neighbours chuno (sabse similar users jinhone item rate kiya hai).
4. Predict karo: neighbours ki ratings ka similarity-weighted average.
5. Sabse high predicted rating wale items recommend karo.

<Sum>

<Given>

| User | I1 | I2 | I3 | I4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alice | 5 | 4 | 1 | ? |
| Bob | 4 | 5 | 1 | 4 |
| Carol | 1 | 2 | 5 | 2 |

</Given>

<Find>

I4 ke liye Alice ki predicted rating.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **I1 se I3 pe cosine similarity** (wo items jo dono ne rate kiye). $|Alice| = \sqrt{25+16+1} = \sqrt{42} \approx 6.481$, $|Bob| = \sqrt{16+25+1} = \sqrt{42} \approx 6.481$, $|Carol| = \sqrt{1+4+25} = \sqrt{30} \approx 5.477$.
2. Alice aur Bob: dot product $= 20 + 20 + 1 = 41$, to $\cos = 41/42 \approx 0.976$.
3. Alice aur Carol: dot product $= 5 + 8 + 5 = 18$, to $\cos = 18/(6.481 \times 5.477) \approx 0.507$.
4. Unki I4 ratings (Bob 4, Carol 2) ka **weighted average**: $\dfrac{0.976 \times 4 + 0.507 \times 2}{0.976 + 0.507} = \dfrac{3.905 + 1.014}{1.483} \approx 3.32$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

Alice ke liye I4 ki predicted rating $\approx 3.3$ (Bob ke 4 ke kareeb, kyunki Bob zyada similar hai).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Weights hain Bob ke liye $0.976/1.483 = 0.658$ aur Carol ke liye $0.342$. Phir $0.658 \times 4 + 0.342 \times 2 = 2.632 + 0.684 = 3.32$. Result 2 aur 4 ke beech hai, jaisa weighted average ko hona chahiye.

</Box>

</Sum>

| | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|
| Collaborative filtering | Item features nahi chahiye, hairaan karne wale items mil sakte hain, zyada users ke saath behtar hota hai | Naye users aur items ke liye cold start, sparse matrix, popularity bias, scale karna mushkil |

**Content-based se comparison:** content-based filtering item features aur user ki apni history use karta hai, jabki CF doosre users ka behaviour use karta hai. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-cf" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-av" />

**Applications:** Netflix, Amazon, YouTube aur Spotify suggestions. **Conclusion:** CF similar users ya similar items ki ratings se predict karta hai ki user ko kya pasand aayega.
