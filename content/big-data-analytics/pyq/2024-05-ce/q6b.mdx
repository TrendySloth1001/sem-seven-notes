**In simple words:** collaborative filtering (CF) recommends items to a user based on what **other similar users** liked. It does not need to know anything about the items themselves, only who rated what. Idea: people who agreed in the past will likely agree again. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-mmds-book" loc="§9.3" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-google-cf" />

**Data used:** the **utility matrix**, with users as rows, items as columns and ratings as cells. Most cells are empty, because a user rates only a few items.

**Types**

1. **User-user CF:** find users similar to the target user, then predict the rating from their ratings.
2. **Item-item CF:** find items similar to the ones the user liked (items rated alike by the same people). Amazon used this. It is more stable, since item tastes change less than user tastes. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-linden2003" />

**Steps (user-user)**

1. Build the utility matrix.
2. Measure similarity between users with cosine similarity or Pearson correlation.
3. Choose the nearest neighbours (most similar users who rated the item).
4. Predict: the similarity-weighted average of the neighbours' ratings.
5. Recommend the items with the highest predicted ratings.

<Sum>

<Given>

| User | I1 | I2 | I3 | I4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alice | 5 | 4 | 1 | ? |
| Bob | 4 | 5 | 1 | 4 |
| Carol | 1 | 2 | 5 | 2 |

</Given>

<Find>

Alice's predicted rating for I4.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. **Cosine similarity on I1 to I3** (items both have rated). $|Alice| = \sqrt{25+16+1} = \sqrt{42} \approx 6.481$, $|Bob| = \sqrt{16+25+1} = \sqrt{42} \approx 6.481$, $|Carol| = \sqrt{1+4+25} = \sqrt{30} \approx 5.477$.
2. Alice and Bob: dot product $= 20 + 20 + 1 = 41$, so $\cos = 41/42 \approx 0.976$.
3. Alice and Carol: dot product $= 5 + 8 + 5 = 18$, so $\cos = 18/(6.481 \times 5.477) \approx 0.507$.
4. **Weighted average** of their I4 ratings (Bob 4, Carol 2): $\dfrac{0.976 \times 4 + 0.507 \times 2}{0.976 + 0.507} = \dfrac{3.905 + 1.014}{1.483} \approx 3.32$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

predicted rating of I4 for Alice $\approx 3.3$ (closer to Bob's 4, because Bob is more similar).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

The weights are $0.976/1.483 = 0.658$ for Bob and $0.342$ for Carol. Then $0.658 \times 4 + 0.342 \times 2 = 2.632 + 0.684 = 3.32$. The result lies between 2 and 4, as a weighted average must.

</Box>

| | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|
| Collaborative filtering | Needs no item features, can find surprising items, improves with more users | Cold start for new users and items, sparse matrix, popularity bias, hard to scale |

**Compared with content-based:** content-based filtering uses item features and the user's own history, while CF uses other users' behaviour. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-cf" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-av" />

**Applications:** Netflix, Amazon, YouTube and Spotify suggestions. **Conclusion:** CF predicts what a user will like from the ratings of similar users or similar items.
