**In simple words:** DGIM stream ke last N bits mein 1s ko bits store kiye bina count karta hai. Ye kuch **buckets** store karta hai, aur buckets ko chhe rules follow karne padte hain. <Cite id="stream-counting-mmds-home" loc="§4.6" /> <Cite id="stream-counting-cs246-slides" />

Bucket ek pair hai (size, apne right end ka timestamp). Size matlab bucket jitne 1s cover karta hai.

**Chhe constraints.**

1. **Bucket ka right end hamesha 1 hota hai.** Bucket kabhi 0 pe khatam nahi hota.
2. **Har 1 kisi na kisi bucket mein hota hai.** Koi 1 bina gine nahi chhoota.
3. **Koi position ek se zyada bucket mein nahi hoti.** Buckets overlap nahi karte.
4. **Har size ke ek ya do buckets hote hain**, ek maximum size tak. Ek size ke teen ho jaayein to merge kar dete hain.
5. **Har size 2 ki power hai:** 1, 2, 4, 8 aur aage.
6. **Purane time ki taraf jaate hue size kam nahi hota.** Newest se oldest tak sizes barabar rehte hain ya badhte hain.

**Example.** Stream (oldest left mein): `1 0 1 1 0 1 1 1`, timestamps 1 se 8. Isme chhe 1s hain, positions 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 aur 8 pe. Buckets, newest pehle, (size, right end) ke roop mein: (1, 8), (1, 7), (2, 6), (2, 3).

| Rule | Check |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Right ends 8, 7, 6 aur 3 sab bits 1 ke barabar hain. |
| 2 and 3 | Buckets 8; 7; 6 aur 4; 3 aur 1 pe ke 1s cover karte hain. Yani saare chhe 1s, har ek ek baar. |
| 4 | Size 1 ke do buckets aur size 2 ke do, to kisi size ke do se zyada nahi. |
| 5 | Sizes 1, 1, 2, 2 powers of 2 hain. |
| 6 | Peeche jaate hue sizes 1, 1, 2, 2 kam nahi hote. |

Sizes ka total 1 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 6, jo 1s ki sankhya ke barabar hai.

**Conclusion:** ye chhe rules buckets ki sankhya chhoti (lagbhag log N) rakhte hain aur phir bhi 50 percent error ke andar count dene dete hain. <Cite id="stream-counting-wiki-dsm" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "DGIM buckets end in a 1, cover every 1 once, come in one or two of each power-of-2 size, and never shrink going back in time."
