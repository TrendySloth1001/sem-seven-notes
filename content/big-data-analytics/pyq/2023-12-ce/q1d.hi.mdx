**In simple words:** DGIM stream ke pichle $N$ bits mein 1's ginta hai, bits ko store kiye bina. Wo kuch **buckets** rakhta hai, har bucket (size, right end ka timestamp) likha jata hai, jahan size us bucket ke 1's ki sankhya hai. Bucket list tabhi valid hai jab wo six constraints follow kare. <Cite id="stream-counting-mmds-home" loc="§4.6" /> <Cite id="stream-counting-cs246-slides" />

**Six constraints**

1. **Bucket ka right end hamesha 1 hota hai.** Bucket kabhi 0 pe khatam nahi hota.
2. **Window ka har 1 kisi na kisi bucket mein hota hai.** Koi 1 uncounted nahi rehta.
3. **Koi position ek se zyada bucket mein nahi hoti.** Buckets overlap nahi karte.
4. **Har size ke ek ya do buckets hote hain**, ek maximum size tak. Ek size ke teen ho jayein to merge karna padta hai.
5. **Har bucket size 2 ki power hai:** 1, 2, 4, 8 aur aage.
6. **Peeche jaate hue sizes kabhi kam nahi hoti.** Newest se oldest tak sizes barabar ya badi hoti hain.

(Jab bucket ka right end pichle $N$ bits ki window se bahar nikal jaye to bucket hata diya jata hai.) <Cite id="stream-counting-wiki-dsm" />

**Example.** Stream (oldest pehle), times 1 se 8: `1 0 1 1 0 1 1 1`. 1's times 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 aur 8 pe hain. Buckets, newest pehle, (size, right end) mein: (1, 8), (1, 7), (2, 6), (2, 3).

| Constraint | Check |
|---|---|
| 1 | Right ends 8, 7, 6 aur 3 sab 1 hain. |
| 2 aur 3 | Buckets 8; 7; 6 aur 4; 3 aur 1 ke 1's cover karte hain. Yani saare chhe 1's, har ek ek baar. |
| 4 | Size 1 ke do aur size 2 ke do buckets, kisi size ke teen nahi. |
| 5 | Sizes 1, 1, 2, 2 sab 2 ki power hain. |
| 6 | Newest se oldest 1, 1, 2, 2 kabhi kam nahi hote. |

Sizes ka total $1 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 6$ hai, yani 1's ki sankhya. Invalid list ka example: 1, 2, 2, 2 (size 2 ke teen buckets) ya 1, 3 (3 power of 2 nahi hai). <Cite id="stream-counting-wiki-streaming" />

**Conclusion:** ye six constraints buckets ki sankhya lagbhag $\log N$ rakhte hain aur phir bhi window count 50 percent error ke andar de dete hain.

Exam mein English mein likho: "In DGIM, every bucket ends in a 1, every 1 is in exactly one bucket, there are one or two buckets of each size, sizes are powers of 2, and sizes never decrease going back in time."
