<Box kind="key">

**Har collaborative filtering question mein ye points likho:**

- <mark>Jo log pehle agree karte the wo aage bhi agree karenge</mark>; items ke baare mein kuch jaanne ki zaroorat nahi.
- Data: <mark>utility matrix</mark>, users x items, cells mein ratings, zyadatar khaali (sparse).
- <mark>User-user CF: similar users dhoondho (cosine ya Pearson), rating ka prediction unki ratings ka similarity-weighted average.</mark>
- <mark>Item-item CF: jo items milte-julte rate hue hon; Amazon use karta hai.</mark>
- Prediction: $r_{xi} = \dfrac{\sum_y s_{xy} r_{yi}}{\sum_y s_{xy}}$, neighbours $y$ pe.
- Limits: <mark>cold start (naye users aur items), sparsity, scalability</mark>.
- Content-based se farq: <mark>collaborative doosre users ki ratings use karta hai; content-based item properties</mark>.

</Box>

## Core answer (5 marks, yahan ke har question ke liye)

<Box kind="definition">

**Collaborative filtering (CF)** recommends items to a user based on what **other, similar users** liked. It does not need to know anything about the items, only who rated what. The idea: "people who agreed in the past will agree in the future". <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-cf" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-google-cf" />

</Box>

**Kyun chahiye:** Flipkart ya Amazon jaisi shop lakhon products bechti hai, aur koi customer sab browse nahi kar sakta. Sahi kuch products recommend karne se sales aur satisfaction badhte hain. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-mmds-book" loc="§9.1" />

**Data:** **utility matrix**: rows users, columns items, cells ratings. Zyadatar cells khaali hote hain kyunki user kuch hi items rate karta hai.

**Steps (user-user CF):** <Cite id="web-bda-2-mmds-recsys" loc="slides 22-27" />

1. Ratings, purchases aur clicks se utility matrix banao.
2. Target user ki baaki sab users se **similarity** nikalo (cosine, ya har user ka average ghatake Pearson correlation).
3. Sabse similar users (**neighbours**) chuno.
4. Missing rating ko neighbours ki us item pe ratings ke similarity-weighted average se **predict** karo.
5. Sabse high predicted ratings wale items **recommend** karo.

**Item-item CF** wahi kaam similar items ke saath karta hai ("customers who bought this also bought that"): milte-julte rate hue items dhoondho aur user ki apni unki ratings ka average lo. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-linden2003" />

```mermaid
%% caption: User-user collaborative filtering. Similar users dhoondho, unhone jo pasand kiya wo target user ko recommend karo.
flowchart LR
  U["Utility matrix: users x items"] --> S["Find users similar to the target user"]
  S --> N["Pick the nearest neighbours"]
  N --> L["Their ratings of items the user has not tried"]
  L --> R["Recommend the highest predicted items"]
```

<Cite id="recommendation-systems-google-cf" loc="diagram" />

**Conclusion:** <mark>collaborative filtering crowd ki ratings se predict karta hai ki user ko kya pasand aayega, aur e-commerce aur movie sites ise products suggest karne ke liye use karti hain</mark>.

## Extended answer (10 marks): ye add karo

### 1. E-commerce example (Flipkart ya Amazon)

(1 se 5 stars, `?` matlab rate nahi kiya)

| User | Phone | Earbuds | Power bank | Laptop bag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asha | 5 | 4 | 5 | ? |
| Ravi | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Meena | 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 |

Asha lagbhag Ravi jaisi rating deti hai (5, 4, 5 vs 5, 5, 4) aur Meena se bahut alag. To Ravi Asha ka neighbour hai. Ravi ne laptop bag ko 5 diya, isliye site Asha ke liye high rating predict karti hai aur use laptop bag dikhati hai. Meena ki raay ko kam weight milta hai. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-gfg-python" />

### 2. Worked example: movie recommendation

<Sum>

<Given>

Ratings (1 se 5); "?" matlab rate nahi kiya.

| | M1 | M2 | M3 | M4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | 5 | 3 | ? | 4 |
| U2 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| U3 | 1 | 5 | 2 | 1 |

</Given>

<Find>

U1 ki M3 ke liye predicted rating, un movies pe cosine similarity se jo dono users ne rate ki hain.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. U1 ke U2 aur U3 ke saath **common movies** M1, M2, M4 hain. U1 = (5, 3, 4), U2 = (4, 2, 4), U3 = (1, 5, 1).
2. **cos(U1, U2).** Dot product $= 20 + 6 + 16 = 42$. Lengths: $\sqrt{50} = 7.071$ aur $\sqrt{36} = 6$. To $42 / (7.071 \times 6) = 0.990$.
3. **cos(U1, U3).** Dot product $= 5 + 15 + 4 = 24$. U3 ki length $= \sqrt{27} = 5.196$. To $24 / (7.071 \times 5.196) = 0.653$.
4. Unki M3 ratings (U2 ne 5, U3 ne 2) ka **weighted average**: $(0.990 \times 5 + 0.653 \times 2) / (0.990 + 0.653) = 6.256 / 1.643 = 3.81$.

</Steps>

<Answer>

U1 ke liye M3 ki predicted rating $\approx 3.81$, isliye M3 ek theek recommendation hai (beech ki rating 3 se upar).

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Weights U2 ke liye 0.602 aur U3 ke liye 0.398 hain, isliye $0.602 \times 5 + 0.398 \times 2 = 3.01 + 0.80 = 3.81$, wahi value. Ye 2 aur 5 ke beech aur 5 ke paas hai, kyunki U2, U1 se zyada similar hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

Raw-rating cosine ye ignore karta hai ki kuch users high rate karte hain aur kuch low; **Pearson correlation** (ratings minus har user ka average) isse theek karta hai. <Cite id="recommendation-systems-wiki-pearson" />

### 3. Content-based filtering se farq

| Point | Content-based | Collaborative filtering |
|---|---|---|
| Kya use karta hai | Item ki **properties** aur user ki apni history | Sirf **kayi users ki ratings** |
| Idea | Aapko jo pasand aaya uske jaise items | Milte-julte rating pattern wale users ya items |
| Item features chahiye | Haan | Nahi |
| Doosre users chahiye | Nahi | Haan, bahut |
| Naya item | Features pata hon to turant recommend ho sakta hai | Jab tak koi rate na kare nahi |
| Naya user | Nahi (profile nahi) | Nahi (history nahi) |
| Variety | Kam, over-specialise | Zyada, surprise kar sakta hai |
| Example | Pandora song attributes match karta hai | Amazon "customers who bought this also bought" |

<Cite id="recommendation-systems-gfg-cb" /> <Cite id="recommendation-systems-av" />

### 4. Advantages aur limitations

| Advantages | Limitations |
|---|---|
| Item details nahi chahiye | **Cold start:** naye users aur items ki ratings nahi |
| Surprising, naye interests dhoondhta hai | **Sparsity:** har user ki kam ratings |
| Kisi bhi tarah ke item ke liye chalta hai | Bahut data chahiye; bade matrices pe slow |

Real systems aksar dono methods milaate hain (**hybrid**).
