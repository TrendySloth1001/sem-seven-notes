**In simple words:** Bitcoin **digital money** hai (peer-to-peer cash system). Ethereum ek **programmable blockchain** hai (world computer) jiska apna paisa, ether, bhi hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-gfg-bitcoin-vs-ethereum" />

| Point | Bitcoin | Ethereum |
|---|---|---|
| Creator and year | Satoshi Nakamoto, 2009 | Vitalik Buterin and others, 2015 |
| Main purpose | Digital cash, store of value | Smart contracts aur dApps ka platform |
| Coin | BTC | ETH |
| Consensus | Proof of Work | Proof of Stake (Merge, 2022 ke baad) |
| Block time | Lagbhag 10 minutes | Lagbhag 12 seconds |
| Ledger model | UTXO (unspent outputs) | Account balance model |
| Scripting | Limited, Turing-complete nahi | EVM pe Solidity, Turing-complete (gas se bounded) |
| Supply | 21 million pe capped | Fixed cap nahi; naya issue kam, fees ka hissa burn |
| Fees | Transaction size (bytes) ke hisaab se | Gas unit ke hisaab se |
| Language | Script | Solidity, Vyper |

<Cite id="bitcoin-educative-bitcoin-vs-ethereum" />

**Similarities:** dono public, permissionless hain, cryptographic hash aur digital signature use karte hain, aur ledger ki copy kai nodes pe rakhte hain.

**Example:** bina bank ke border paar paisa bhejna ho to Bitcoin. Lending app chalana ya NFT banana ho, jahan contract rules enforce kare, to Ethereum.

**Conclusion:** Bitcoin secure aur simple money pe focus karta hai; Ethereum kisi bhi application ke liye flexible platform banne pe. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" /> <Cite id="ethereum-whitepaper" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "Bitcoin is a digital currency, whereas Ethereum is a programmable platform for smart contracts."
