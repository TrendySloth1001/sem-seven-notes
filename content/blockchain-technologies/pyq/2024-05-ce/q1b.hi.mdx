**In simple words:** wallet coins hold nahi karta. Wo **private keys** rakhta hai jinse tum blockchain pe likhe coins kharch kar sakte ho. Fark is baat ka hai ki keys **internet se connected** device pe hain (hot) ya **offline** hain (cold). <Cite id="wallets-utxo-double-spending-gfg-hot-cold" />

**Hot wallet:** keys phone, laptop, browser extension ya exchange server pe hoti hain jo online hai. Examples: MetaMask, mobile app, exchange account. Daily use ke liye fast aur easy, lekin hacker, malware ya phishing site keys tak pahunch sakte hain. <Cite id="wallets-utxo-double-spending-wiki-crypto-wallet" />

**Cold wallet:** keys offline banti aur store hoti hain: hardware wallet (Ledger, Trezor), paper wallet, ya air-gapped computer. Transaction offline sign hota hai aur sirf signed result network ko bheja jaata hai. Online attackers keys tak nahi pahunch sakte. <Cite id="wallets-utxo-double-spending-btcwiki-cold" /> <Cite id="wallets-utxo-double-spending-wiki-hw-wallet" />

| Point | Hot wallet | Cold wallet |
|---|---|---|
| Internet | Hamesha ya aksar connected | Offline |
| Security | Kam: hacking aur malware ka khatra | Zyada: online attacks se safe |
| Convenience | Bahut easy, instant payment | Slow: connect ya offline sign karna padta hai |
| Best for | Chhoti amount, daily kharch, trading | Badi amount, long-term saving |
| Cost | Zyada tar free | Hardware ka paisa lagta hai |
| Main risk | Hack, phishing | Device kho jaana, chori, kharab hona, backup lost |

**Example:** pocket money hot wallet mein rakho aur savings cold wallet mein, jaise jeb mein cash aur bank locker mein gold.

**Conclusion:** hot wallet convenience deta hai, cold wallet security. Dono mein **seed phrase** backup safe rakhna zaroori hai, kyunki jiske paas keys hain wahi coins ka maalik hai. <Cite id="wallets-utxo-double-spending-mb-ch05-wallets" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "A hot wallet keeps the private keys online; a cold wallet keeps them offline."
