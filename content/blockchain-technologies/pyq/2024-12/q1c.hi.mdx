**In simple words:** mining difficulty batati hai ki valid block dhoondhna kitna mushkil hai. Network ise adjust karta rehta hai taaki naya block lagbhag same speed se aaye, chahe zyada ya kam miners judein.

**Definition:** proof of work mein miner ek number (nonce) badalta rehta hai jab tak block header ka hash ek **target** se chhota na ho jaye. Chhota target matlab mushkil puzzle. Difficulty batati hai ki current target sabse aasan target se kitna chhota hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-btcwiki-difficulty" />

**Kyun zaroori hai:** agar zyada miners aayein to blocks tez aur tez aate. Fixed block time (Bitcoin mein lagbhag 10 minutes) money supply aur confirmation time ko steady rakhta hai. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" loc="§4" />

**Kaise adjust hota hai (Bitcoin):**

1. Har 2016 blocks ke baad, har node actual time ko expected time (2016 x 10 minutes = 2 weeks) se compare karta hai.
2. New difficulty = old difficulty x (expected time / actual time). Change 4 guna upar ya neeche tak limited hai.
3. Expected se tez matlab difficulty badhti hai; dheere matlab ghatti hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-mb-ch12-mining" />

<Sum>

<Given>

Pichhle 2016 blocks 10 din mein bane. Expected time 14 din hai. Old difficulty $D$ hai.

</Given>

<Find>

new difficulty.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. Ratio $= \text{expected} / \text{actual} = 14 / 10 = 1.4$. *Reason: blocks bahut tez aaye, isliye scale up karte hain.*
2. New difficulty $= D \times 1.4$. *Reason: formula old value ko ratio se multiply karta hai.*

</Steps>

<Answer>

new difficulty $= 1.4\,D$ (40% mushkil)

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

$1.4\,D$ pe har block 1.4 guna zyada time leta hai: 2016 blocks ke liye $10 \times 1.4 = 14$ din, jo 2-week ka goal hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Effect:** zyada hash power matlab zyada difficulty aur zyada energy use, aur isse attack mehenga ho jaata hai. <Cite id="consensus-mining-gfg-pow" />
