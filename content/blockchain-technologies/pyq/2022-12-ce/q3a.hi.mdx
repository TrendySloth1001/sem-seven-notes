**In simple words:** Bitcoin digital paisa hai. Ethereum blockchain pe ek computer hai jo programs chala sakta hai, aur uska apna paisa (ether) bhi hai. Mining difficulty yeh hai ki Bitcoin mein valid block hash dhundhna kitna mushkil hai.

**Comparison of Bitcoin and Ethereum:**

| Point | Bitcoin | Ethereum |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Peer-to-peer digital cash | Smart contracts aur dApps ka platform |
| Creator | Satoshi Nakamoto (2009) | Vitalik Buterin aur others (2015) |
| Coin | BTC | ETH (ether) |
| Consensus | Proof of Work (SHA-256) | Merge (September 2022) ke baad Proof of Stake |
| Block time | Lagbhag 10 minutes | 12-second slots |
| Data model | UTXO | Account model with a world state |
| Programming | Limited Script | Solidity, Turing-complete EVM |
| Fees | Transaction ke per byte fee | Gas used x gas price |
| Supply | 21 million pe capped | Koi fixed cap nahi |

**Bitcoin mein mining difficulty.** Miner ko aisa nonce dhundhna hota hai ki block hash ek **target number se chhota** ho. Chhota target matlab kam valid hashes, yaani zyada mushkil. Difficulty current target ko sabse aasan possible target (target_1) se compare karti hai:

$$
\text{Difficulty} = \frac{\text{target}_1}{\text{current target}}
$$

Total hash power kuch bhi ho, har block lagbhag 10 minute mein aaye isliye network **har 2016 blocks pe retarget** karta hai (lagbhag 2 hafte, kyunki 2016 x 10 min = 14 days):

$$
\text{new difficulty} = \text{old difficulty} \times \frac{14\ \text{days}}{\text{actual time for the last 2016 blocks}}
$$

Blocks 10 minute se jaldi aaye to difficulty badhti hai. Der se aaye to kam ho jaati hai. Ek baar mein change ki limit 4 ka factor hai. Average mein ek miner ko ek block ke liye lagbhag difficulty x 2^32 hashes chahiye. <Cite id="bitcoin-btcwiki-difficulty" /> <Cite id="bitcoin-mb-ch12-mining" />

<Sum>

<Given>

old difficulty $= 1{,}000{,}000$; pichle 2016 blocks plan ke 14 days ki jagah 10 days mein bane.

</Given>

<Find>

new difficulty aur ek block ke liye average hashes ki sankhya.

</Find>

<Steps>

1. Blocks bahut jaldi aaye, to difficulty badhni chahiye. *Reason: 10 days, 14 days se kam hain.*
2. Ratio $= 14 / 10 = 1.4$. *Reason: planned time ko actual time se divide karo.*
3. New difficulty $= 1{,}000{,}000 \times 1.4 = 1{,}400{,}000$.
4. Hashes per block $\approx 1{,}400{,}000 \times 2^{32} = 1{,}400{,}000 \times 4{,}294{,}967{,}296 \approx 6.01 \times 10^{15}$. *Reason: difficulty ki har unit ko lagbhag $2^{32}$ hashes chahiye.*

</Steps>

<Answer>

new difficulty $= 1{,}400{,}000$, aur ek block ke liye lagbhag $6.01 \times 10^{15}$ hashes.

</Answer>

<Box kind="verify">

Wahi miners kaam karte rahein to ab 2016 blocks mein $10 \times 1.4 = 14$ days lagenge, jo planned time hai. Ratio 1.4 allowed limit 4 ke andar bhi hai.

</Box>

</Sum>

**Conclusion:** Bitcoin simple, secure money network hai; Ethereum programmable platform hai. Bitcoin har 2016 blocks pe difficulty badal kar apni speed banaye rakhta hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "Difficulty = target_1 / current target, and it is adjusted every 2016 blocks so that a block is mined about every 10 minutes."
