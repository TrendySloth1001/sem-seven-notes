**In simple words:** blockchain records ki ek shared notebook hai jiski copy bahut saare computers rakhte hain. Purane page ko koi chupke se badal nahi sakta, kyunki har page apne pichle page se locked hota hai.

**Definition:** blockchain ek distributed, append-only ledger hai. Transactions ko blocks mein group karte hain, aur har block pichle block ka hash store karta hai, isliye blocks ek chain bana lete hain. Network agla block consensus rule se agree karta hai, to koi ek party record ko control nahi karti. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" /> <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ibm-blockchain" />

**Types of blockchain** is baat mein alag hote hain ki kaun read, write aur validate kar sakta hai:

- **Public:** koi bhi join, read aur validate kar sakta hai (Bitcoin, Ethereum).
- **Private:** ek organisation ki hoti hai aur wahi decide karti hai kaun join karega (company ka internal ledger).
- **Consortium:** kuch known organisations milke control share karti hain (bank group jo Hyperledger Fabric use karta hai).
- **Hybrid:** thoda hissa public, thoda private, to kuch data hidden rehta hai.

| Point | Public | Private | Consortium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who can join | Anyone | Ek owner invite karta hai | Group invite karta hai |
| Control | Koi nahi (decentralised) | Ek organisation | Kai organisations |
| Consensus | PoW, PoS (slow, costly) | Raft, PBFT (fast) | PBFT, Raft (fast) |
| Speed | Low | High | High |
| Privacy | Low, sab dikhta hai | High | Medium to high |
| Trust needed | Kisi pe nahi | Owner pe trust | Group pe trust |
| Example | Bitcoin | Internal audit ledger | Hyperledger Fabric network |

<Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-gfg-types" /> <Cite id="private-blockchain-gfg-pubpriv" />

**Conclusion:** public chain openness deti hai, private chain speed aur privacy deti hai, aur consortium chain dono ke beech mein hoti hai.

Exam mein English mein likho: "A blockchain is a distributed, append-only ledger of hash-linked blocks that the network agrees on by consensus."
