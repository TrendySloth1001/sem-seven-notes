**In simple words:** blockchain sounds perfect, but putting it into real use is hard. Five main challenges are below. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-gfg-advantages" />

1. **Scalability:** Bitcoin handles only about 7 transactions per second, Ethereum about 15 to 30, while Visa handles thousands. Every node stores and checks everything, so the network is slow when busy.
2. **High energy use:** Proof of Work chains use huge electricity (Bitcoin uses about as much electricity per year as some mid-sized countries). <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ccaf-cbeci" />
3. **Security and privacy:** 51% attacks, smart contract bugs and lost private keys (lost keys mean lost money, no reset). Public data on the chain also clashes with privacy rules.
4. **Regulation and legal doubt:** laws about crypto, data deletion (GDPR "right to be forgotten" versus immutability) and who is liable differ by country.
5. **Integration and skills:** old systems must be connected, standards differ between chains (interoperability), and skilled developers are few.

**Other points:** storage grows forever, data cannot be corrected once written, and users find it hard to use. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nist-8202" />

**Conclusion:** the technology works, but scalability, energy, security, law and skills must be solved before wide adoption. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ibm-blockchain" />
