**In simple words:** dono ye tareeke hain jisse ajnabi log agle block pe agree karte hain. **Proof of Work (PoW)** mein miners bijli kharch karke ek puzzle solve karte hain. **Proof of Stake (PoS)** mein validators apne khud ke coins lock (stake) karte hain, aur network unmein se ek ko agla block propose karne ke liye chunta hai. <Cite id="consensus-mining-wiki-pow" />

| Point | Proof of Work | Proof of Stake |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes blocks | Miners | Validators |
| What you risk | Hardware and electricity cost | Your staked coins (can be slashed) |
| Selection | First to find a valid hash | Chosen by stake and randomness |
| Energy use | Very high | Very low (about 99% less for Ethereum) |
| Hardware | ASICs, GPUs | Ordinary computer |
| Speed | Slower | Faster finality |
| Main attack | 51% of hash power | Owning a large share of all staked coins |
| Risk | Mining centralised in big pools | Rich get richer |
| Examples | Bitcoin | Ethereum (since 2022), Cardano |

<Cite id="consensus-mining-eth-pos" />

**Conclusion:** PoW proven aur bahut secure hai par energy waste karta hai; PoS greener aur faster hai par naya hai aur bade holders ko favour karta hai. <Cite id="consensus-mining-gfg-pos" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "PoW depends on computing power; PoS depends on the coins a validator stakes."
