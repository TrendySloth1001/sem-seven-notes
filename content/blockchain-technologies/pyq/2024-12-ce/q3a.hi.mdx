**In simple words:** cryptocurrency digital paisa hai jo sirf blockchain pe exist karta hai. Ye cryptography se protected hota hai, koi central bank nahi hota, aur ownership private key se prove hoti hai. Bitcoin (2009) pehla tha. <Cite id="cryptocurrencies-tokens-wiki-crypto" />

**Features:** decentralised, secure (cryptography), transparent public ledger, borderless, pseudonymous, aur aksar limited supply.

**How it works (short):** aapke paas keys wala wallet hota hai; aap transaction sign karte hain; miners ya validators use check karke block mein daalte hain; naya balance sabke liye record ho jaata hai.

**Types of cryptocurrencies:**

1. **Coins (native coins):** apni khud ki blockchain pe chalte hain aur uske use ka payment karte hain. Examples: Bitcoin (BTC), Ether (ETH), Litecoin (LTC). Bitcoin mostly store of value hai; Litecoin faster payment coin hai. <Cite id="cryptocurrencies-tokens-gfg-coins-tokens" />
2. **Altcoins:** Bitcoin ke alawa har coin, jaise Litecoin, Ripple (XRP) aur Cardano (ADA).
3. **Tokens:** dusri blockchain pe smart contract se bane hote hain, apni chain nahi hoti. ERC-20 tokens (fungible, jaise USDT, LINK) aur ERC-721 (non-fungible, NFTs). Utility tokens service ka access dete hain; security tokens ownership dikhate hain, jaise shares. <Cite id="cryptocurrencies-tokens-eth-erc20" />
4. **Stablecoins:** value ek steady asset se bandhi hoti hai jaise US dollar (USDC, USDT, DAI), isliye price zyada nahi hilti. <Cite id="cryptocurrencies-tokens-wiki-stablecoin" />
5. **Privacy coins:** sender, receiver aur amount chhupate hain, jaise Monero aur Zcash.
6. **Meme coins:** jokes ya social trends se shuru hue, jaise Dogecoin; bahut risky.
7. **Central bank digital currencies (CBDC):** government ka jaari kiya digital paisa; India ka e-rupee example hai. Ye decentralised cryptocurrency nahi hai, par related hai.

| Type | Own blockchain | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Coin | Yes | BTC, ETH |
| Token | No (uses another chain) | USDT, LINK |
| Stablecoin | Usually token | USDC, DAI |

**Conclusion:** cryptocurrencies money (coins) se application assets (tokens) aur price-stable coins tak hoti hain, har ek alag zaroorat ke liye.

Exam mein English mein likho: "A cryptocurrency is a digital currency secured by cryptography and recorded on a blockchain."
