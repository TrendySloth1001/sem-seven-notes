**In simple words:** blockchain sunne mein perfect lagta hai, par real use mein laana mushkil hai. Paanch main challenges neeche hain. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-gfg-advantages" />

1. **Scalability:** Bitcoin sirf lagbhag 7 transactions per second handle karta hai, Ethereum lagbhag 15 se 30, jabki Visa hazaaron handle karta hai. Har node sab kuch store aur check karta hai, isliye busy hone par network slow ho jaata hai.
2. **High energy use:** Proof of Work chains bahut bijli use karte hain (Bitcoin saal mein kuch mid-sized deshon jitni bijli use karta hai). <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ccaf-cbeci" />
3. **Security and privacy:** 51% attacks, smart contract bugs aur khoyi hui private keys (key gayi to paisa gaya, koi reset nahi). Chain pe public data privacy rules se bhi takrata hai.
4. **Regulation and legal doubt:** crypto ke laws, data deletion (GDPR ka "right to be forgotten" versus immutability) aur liability kisski, ye desh ke hisaab se alag hai.
5. **Integration and skills:** purane systems ko jodna padta hai, chains ke beech standards alag hain (interoperability), aur skilled developers kam hain.

**Other points:** storage hamesha badhta rehta hai, ek baar likha data sudhara nahi ja sakta, aur users ke liye use karna mushkil hai. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nist-8202" />

**Conclusion:** technology kaam karti hai, par wide adoption se pehle scalability, energy, security, law aur skills solve karne honge. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ibm-blockchain" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "The main challenges are scalability, energy use, security and privacy, regulation, and integration."
