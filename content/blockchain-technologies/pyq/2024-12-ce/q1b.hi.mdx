**In simple words:** Bitcoin ek blockchain hai jo digital money banne ke liye bana hai. Ethereum ek blockchain hai jo "world computer" banne ke liye bana hai, jo programs (smart contracts) chalata hai, aur uska apna coin Ether hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-gfg-bitcoin-vs-ethereum" />

| Point | Bitcoin | Ethereum |
|---|---|---|
| Creator and year | Satoshi Nakamoto, 2009 <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" /> | Vitalik Buterin and others, 2015 <Cite id="ethereum-whitepaper" /> |
| Main purpose | Peer-to-peer digital cash, store of value | Platform for smart contracts and dApps |
| Coin | BTC | ETH (Ether) |
| Programming | Limited Script language, not Turing complete | Solidity on the EVM, Turing complete |
| Account model | UTXO (unspent outputs) | Account balances (EOA and contract accounts) |
| Consensus | Proof of Work | Proof of Stake (since the Merge, 2022) |
| Block time | About 10 minutes | About 12 seconds |
| Supply | Capped at 21 million | No fixed cap |
| Fees | Fee by transaction size | Gas, by computation used |

**Conclusion:** Bitcoin safe aur simple money pe focus karta hai; Ethereum programmable applications pe, thodi zyada complexity ke saath. <Cite id="bitcoin-educative-bitcoin-vs-ethereum" />

Exam mein English mein likho: "Bitcoin is digital money; Ethereum is a programmable platform for smart contracts."
