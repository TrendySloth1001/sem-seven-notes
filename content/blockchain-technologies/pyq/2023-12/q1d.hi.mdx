**In simple words:** private blockchain wo blockchain hai jisme sirf invited, jaane-pehchaane members hi join kar sakte hain. Companies aur banks ko ye isliye chahiye kyunki fully public chain privacy, speed aur control nahi deti. <Cite id="private-blockchain-gfg-private" />

**Private blockchain ki need kyun hai:**

1. **Business data ki privacy.** Public chain pe har transaction sabko dikhta hai. Company apne prices, contracts ya customers competitors ko nahi dikha sakti. Private chain data sirf members ko dikhati hai.
2. **Known identities.** Rules (KYC jaise laws aur audit rules) ko pata hona chahiye ki kaun part le raha hai. Members identify hote hain aur permission paate hain.
3. **High speed aur low cost.** Sirf kuch trusted nodes agree karte hain, to mining ki jagah light consensus (Raft ya PBFT) use ho sakta hai. Isse high throughput milta hai aur mining fees nahi lagti.
4. **Control aur governance.** Owner ya consortium decide karta hai ki kaun read, write aur validate karega, aur rules badal sakta hai ya errors fix kar sakta hai.
5. **Legal compliance.** Data rules (jaise data ek country ke andar rahe) follow ho sakte hain, kyunki nodes known hote hain.
6. **Kam energy.** Proof-of-work mining ki zaroorat nahi.

| | Public | Private |
|---|---|---|
| Kaun join kar sakta hai | Anyone | Invited members |
| Speed | Slow | Fast |
| Privacy | Low | High |
| Consensus | PoW / PoS | PBFT, Raft |

<Cite id="private-blockchain-gfg-pubpriv" />

**Examples:** Hyperledger Fabric, Corda aur Quorum trade finance aur supply chains mein use hote hain. <Cite id="private-blockchain-fabric-whatis" />

**Limitation:** ye kam decentralised hai, to members ko owners pe zyada bharosa karna padta hai.

**Conclusion:** private blockchain shared-ledger ke fayde deti hai aur saath mein privacy, speed aur control bhi, jo businesses ko chahiye.
