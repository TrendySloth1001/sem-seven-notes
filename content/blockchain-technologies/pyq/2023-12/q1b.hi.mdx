**In simple words:** Bitcoin ek digital money hai jo bank ke bina chalta hai. Log seedha ek dusre ko coins bhejte hain, aur computers ka network har payment check karke record karta hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-wiki-bitcoin" />

**Bitcoin kya hai?** Ye pehli cryptocurrency hai, jise Satoshi Nakamoto ne 2008 mein describe kiya aur 2009 mein start kiya. Ye ek peer-to-peer electronic cash system hai. Payments ek public blockchain mein store hote hain, cryptography (hashes aur digital signatures) se protected hote hain, aur naye blocks miners proof of work se add karte hain. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" />

**Hash cash kya hai?** Hashcash ek proof-of-work idea hai jo Adam Back ne (1997 mein propose kiya, paper 2002 mein) email spam rokne ke liye banaya. Sender ko har email ke liye thoda computer work karna padta hai: ek number (nonce) dhoondhna jisse email header ka hash fixed number of zero bits se start ho. Ye work karna costly hai, lekin check karna sirf ek hash ka kaam hai. Normal user ko lagbhag kuch nahi lagta, lekin lakhon mails bhejne wale spammer ko bahut kharch padta hai. <Cite id="bitcoin-hashcash-back" />

**Bitcoin mein hash cash ka role:**

1. Bitcoin yahi idea apne **proof of work** ke liye use karta hai. Miner block header mein nonce badalta rehta hai jab tak header ka SHA-256 hash target se chhota na aa jaye.
2. Isse block add karna **expensive** ho jaata hai, to cheating mein asli bijli aur hardware lagta hai.
3. **Longest chain** fake karna mushkil ho jaata hai: attacker ko har block ka work dobara karna padega.
4. Checking sasti hai: baaki nodes ek hash se verify kar lete hain.
5. Ye control karta hai ki agla block kaun banayega aur kitni speed se, kyunki target (difficulty) adjust hota hai taaki lagbhag har 10 minute mein ek block aaye.

| | Hashcash | Bitcoin PoW (2009) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Spam email rokna | Agle block pe agree karna |
| Work kis pe | Email header | Block header |
| Hash | SHA-1 | Double SHA-256 |

<Cite id="bitcoin-wiki-hashcash" /> <Cite id="bitcoin-gfg-pow" />

**Conclusion:** Bitcoin peer-to-peer digital cash hai, aur hash cash use wo work deta hai jo banana costly aur check karna easy hai, jisse chain secure rehti hai.
