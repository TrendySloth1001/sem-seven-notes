**In simple words:** blockchain bahut saare computers dwara rakha gaya ek shared, badalne mein mushkil record hai. Is design ki wajah se ise kuch strong benefits milte hain, aur kuch real limits bhi hain. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ibm-blockchain" />

**Benefits**

1. **Decentralisation.** Data ko koi ek bank ya company control nahi karti, to single point of failure nahi hai.
2. **Transparency.** Saare permitted participants same records dekhte aur verify karte hain.
3. **Immutability.** Har block mein previous block ka hash hota hai, to purana record badalne se baad ke saare blocks toot jaate hain. Ye practically tamper-proof hai. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" />
4. **Security.** Cryptography (hashes, digital signatures) data aur ownership ko protect karti hai.
5. **Traceability.** Har transaction time-stamped aur linked hai, jo supply chains aur audits mein kaam aata hai.
6. **Kam cost aur fast settlement.** Middlemen nahi, to fees aur reconciliation steps kam.
7. **Automation.** Smart contracts agreements ko apne aap chalate hain.
8. **Strangers ke beech trust.** Rules network enforce karta hai, koi insaan nahi.

**Limitations**

1. **Scalability.** Bitcoin sirf kuch transactions per second (lagbhag 7) handle karta hai, jabki card networks hazaaron.
2. **Proof of work mein high energy use.** Bitcoin mining mein ek mid-sized country jitni bijli lagti hai. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ccaf-cbeci" />
3. **Storage growth.** Har full node poori history store karta hai.
4. **Immutability dono taraf kaam karti hai.** Smart contract ki galti ya bug aasani se undo nahi hota, aur private key kho gayi to coins kho gaye.
5. **Privacy issues.** Public chains saare transactions dikhati hain (pseudonymous, anonymous nahi), jo "right to be forgotten" jaise rules se takraata hai.
6. **Chhoti chains pe 51% attack ka risk.**
7. **Regulation** kai countries mein clear nahi hai, aur purane systems ke saath **integration** mushkil hai.
8. **Skill gap aur complexity.**

| Benefit | Matching limitation |
|---|---|
| Decentralised, no middleman | Slow, kyunki kai nodes ko agree karna padta hai |
| Transparent | Kam privacy |
| Immutable | Errors fix nahi ho sakte |
| Secure | Zyada energy (PoW) |

<Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-gfg-advantages" />

**Conclusion:** blockchain wahan best hai jahan trust, transparency aur traceability raw speed aur privacy se zyada important hain. Ye har problem ka sahi tool nahi hai. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-wiki-blockchain" />
