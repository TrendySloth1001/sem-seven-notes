**In simple words:** private blockchain mein saare validators known aur approved hote hain, to costly mining ki zaroorat nahi. Nodes simply **vote** karke agree ho sakte hain. Ye faster hai aur lagbhag koi energy nahi lagti. <Cite id="private-blockchain-gfg-private" />

**Yahan voting kyun chalti hai:** nodes kam hain aur unki identity known hai, to network votes gin sakta hai aur kharab node ko pehchaan kar hata sakta hai.

**Common methods**

1. **Raft (crash fault tolerant).** Ek *leader* elect hota hai. Clients commands leader ko bhejte hain, jo unhe *followers* tak copy karta hai. Majority confirm kare to entry commit ho jaati hai. Leader fail ho to nayi election hoti hai. Ye maanta hai ki nodes crash ho sakte hain par jhooth nahi bolte, to f failures survive karne ke liye n kam se kam 2f + 1 chahiye. Hyperledger Fabric ki ordering service mein use hota hai. <Cite id="bft-paxos-raft-raft-paper" /> <Cite id="bft-paxos-raft-fabric-orderer" />
2. **Paxos.** Purana crash-tolerant protocol, wahi majority idea (proposers, acceptors, learners). Samajhna mushkil hai, isliye Raft ek aasan alternative ke roop mein bana.
3. **PBFT (Byzantine fault tolerant).** Faulty ya malicious nodes ko bhi handle karta hai. Nodes teen phases (pre-prepare, prepare, commit) se guzarte hain aur n kam se kam 3f + 1 chahiye. Instant finality deta hai. Network messages n squared ki tarah badhte hain, to chhote groups ke liye suit karta hai. <Cite id="bft-paxos-raft-gfg-pbft" />
4. **IBFT / QBFT.** Quorum jaisi Ethereum-based private chains ke liye PBFT-style voting. <Cite id="corda-ripple-quorum-quorum-ibft" />
5. **Proof of Authority.** Approved validators baari-baari blocks sign karte hain.

| | Raft | PBFT |
|---|---|---|
| Faults handled | Crash only | Crash aur malicious |
| f faults ke liye nodes | 2f + 1 | 3f + 1 |
| Example | 3 nodes 1 crash survive karte hain | 4 nodes 1 bad node survive karte hain |
| Speed | Very fast | Fast, zyada nodes pe slow |

**Conclusion:** private chains mining ki jagah known nodes ke beech voting use karti hain. Agar members ke jhooth na bolne pe bharosa hai to Raft, aur agar kuch malicious ho sakte hain to PBFT chuno.
