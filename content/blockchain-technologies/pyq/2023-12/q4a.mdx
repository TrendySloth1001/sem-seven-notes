**In simple words:** a blockchain is a shared, hard-to-change record kept by many computers. Because of that design it gives some strong benefits, and it also has real limits. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ibm-blockchain" />

**Benefits**

1. **Decentralisation.** No single bank or company controls the data, so there is no single point of failure.
2. **Transparency.** All permitted participants see the same records and can verify them.
3. **Immutability.** Each block holds the hash of the previous block, so changing an old record breaks every later block. It is effectively tamper-proof. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-nakamoto" />
4. **Security.** Cryptography (hashes, digital signatures) protects data and ownership.
5. **Traceability.** Every transaction is time-stamped and linked, which helps in supply chains and audits.
6. **Lower cost and faster settlement.** No middlemen, so fewer fees and fewer reconciliation steps.
7. **Automation.** Smart contracts run agreements automatically.
8. **Trust between strangers.** The rules are enforced by the network, not by a person.

**Limitations**

1. **Scalability.** Bitcoin handles only a few transactions per second (about 7), while card networks handle thousands.
2. **High energy use** in proof of work. Bitcoin mining uses electricity comparable to a mid-sized country. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-ccaf-cbeci" />
3. **Storage growth.** Every full node stores the entire history.
4. **Immutability cuts both ways.** A mistake or a bug in a smart contract cannot be undone easily, and lost private keys mean lost coins.
5. **Privacy issues.** Public chains show all transactions (pseudonymous, not anonymous), and this conflicts with rules like "right to be forgotten".
6. **51% attack risk** on small chains.
7. **Regulation is unclear** in many countries, and **integration** with old systems is hard.
8. **Skill gap and complexity.**

| Benefit | Matching limitation |
|---|---|
| Decentralised, no middleman | Slow, since many nodes must agree |
| Transparent | Less privacy |
| Immutable | Errors cannot be fixed |
| Secure | Uses much energy (PoW) |

<Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-gfg-advantages" />

**Conclusion:** blockchain is best where trust, transparency and traceability matter more than raw speed and privacy. It is not the right tool for every problem. <Cite id="blockchain-fundamentals-wiki-blockchain" />
